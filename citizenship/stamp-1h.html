Stamp 1H Ireland explained

Hospital doctors (NCHDs) on a multi-site permit.

✅ Counts towards citizenship

Checked against ISD guidance: November 2026

What is Stamp 1H?

Stamp 1H is for doctors holding a two-year multi-site General Employment Permit to work as a Non-Consultant Hospital Doctor (NCHD) in public hospitals or health facilities.

Who gets Stamp 1H?

  • NCHDs with a two-year multi-site General Employment Permit

Main conditions

  • Your employer must tell the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment about changes of location or contract before they happen
  • You can move to a different hospital within the HSE group at the end of your initial contract, with a valid contract
  • Conditions similar to Critical Skills Employment Permit holders apply, including for your spouse or family

Can I work on Stamp 1H? Yes, as an NCHD under your permit.

Does Stamp 1H count towards Irish citizenship?

Yes. ISD confirms Stamp 1H is reckonable residence when applying for citizenship by naturalisation.

Tips if you're planning to apply for citizenship

  • Doctors can use a Medical Practitioner Employment History Summary as proof of residence
  • Renew on time so there are no gaps between permissions

When can you apply?
