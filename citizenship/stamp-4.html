Stamp 4 Ireland explained

Long-term residence, spouses of Irish citizens, refugees and more.

✅ Counts towards citizenship

Checked against ISD guidance: November 2026

What is Stamp 4?

Stamp 4 is permission to stay in Ireland for a set period with the right to work without an employment permit and to set up a business. It's one of the most common routes to citizenship.

Who gets Stamp 4?

  • People who held a Critical Skills Employment Permit for 2 years, another employment permit for 5 years, or were a researcher on a Hosting Agreement for 2 years
  • Spouses, civil partners or de facto partners of Irish citizens
  • Recognised refugees, people with subsidiary protection, and family joining them
  • Parents of an Irish citizen child
  • Investor and Entrepreneur Programme participants and their families
  • Long-term residence permission holders

Main conditions

  • You can work without an employment permit and work in a profession (subject to professional body rules)
  • You can set up and run a business
  • You may access State funds and services as set by Government departments
  • Renew your permission and registration before they expire

Can I work on Stamp 4? Yes, without an employment permit, and you can run a business.

Does Stamp 4 count towards Irish citizenship?

Yes. ISD confirms Stamp 4 is reckonable residence for citizenship by naturalisation.

Tips if you're planning to apply for citizenship

  • Spouses of Irish citizens may qualify after 3 years' residence (with 3 years of marriage) – choose the spouse route in the calculator
  • Refugees can apply 5 years from the exact date international protection was granted
  • Keep renewing on time – a gap breaks the continuous year before you apply
