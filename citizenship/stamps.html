Irish immigration stamps explained

Every Irish immigration stamp explained in plain English – who gets it, what you can do on it, and whether it counts towards citizenship by naturalisation.

❌ Does not count

✅ Counts towards citizenship

✅ Counts towards citizenship

✅ Counts towards citizenship

Stamp 6

➖ Not applicable

What doesn't count as reckonable residence

  • Time on Stamp 2 or Stamp 2A (students)
  • Time on Stamp 0 (not included in ISD's residency calculator)
  • Time as an international protection (asylum) applicant – refugees can apply 5 years from the date protection was granted
  • Any period without a valid, registered permission – for example waiting too long to renew
  • Time living in Ireland unlawfully
Keep your permission registered with no gaps. ISD warns that gaps in registration can make a citizenship application ineligible.
