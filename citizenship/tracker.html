The stages of a citizenship application

  1. Apply online and pay €175. Upload certified copies of your identity (150 points) and residence proofs (150 points per year).
  2. Application number and e-vetting link. You get an application number and a link to complete Garda e-vetting.
  3. Checks and possible requests. ISD may ask for more documents or ask you to explain information in your application.
  4. Approval letter. ISD tells you the Minister intends to grant a certificate of naturalisation. You pay the certification fee (€950 for adults) and upload a copy of your IRP and travel document if you're from outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK.
  5. Citizenship ceremony. You're invited to a ceremony to make your declaration of fidelity. It's mandatory for adults.
  6. Certificate by post. Your certificate of naturalisation arrives in the weeks after the ceremony – then you can apply for an Irish passport.

If your address or email changes during the process, you must tell ISD's Citizenship Division and give proof of the new address.
