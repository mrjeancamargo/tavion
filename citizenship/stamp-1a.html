Stamp 1A Ireland explained

Trainee accountants.

✅ Counts towards citizenship

Checked against ISD guidance: November 2026

What is Stamp 1A?

Stamp 1A is permission to take part in full-time, paid accountancy training in Ireland with a training contract with a company based here, for a set period.

Who gets Stamp 1A?

  • Non-EU/EEA trainee accountants in full-time, paid training under the immigration rules for trainee accountants

Main conditions

  • No other business, trade or profession unless your ISD letter allows it
  • This is a time-limited permission – your accountancy course must be completed within 4 years
  • Renew before expiry, or apply for another permission such as an employment permit

Can I work on Stamp 1A? Only in your accountancy traineeship.

Does Stamp 1A count towards Irish citizenship?

Yes. Stamp 1A is one of the reckonable permissions in ISD's naturalisation residency calculator, so time on Stamp 1A counts towards citizenship.

Tips if you're planning to apply for citizenship

  • Plan your next permission before your Stamp 1A ends so there is no gap
  • Keep proof of residence for every year (for example employment summaries and bank statements)
