Irish Citizenship Calculator

Work out your reckonable residence and the earliest date you can apply for Irish citizenship by naturalisation. Free, no sign-up, and your dates never leave your phone.

Add holidays and trips abroad, especially in the last 12 months. Travel days count as days in Ireland.

🔒 Your dates are saved only on this device so you can come back and update them. Nothing is sent to us.

How the calculator works

  1. Reckonable days. We count every day covered by a permission that counts, in the 9 years (or 5 for spouses) up to your application date. Overlapping permissions are only counted once – the same way ISD's own residency calculator works.
  2. Continuous year. Your permissions must cover the full year immediately before you apply with no gaps.
  3. Absences. You can be abroad for up to 70 full days in that final year (up to 100 if ISD accepts exceptional circumstances).
  4. Earliest date. We check every day ahead to find the first date all three conditions are met.
