How CAO points work

  1. Your best six subjects count, from one sitting of the Leaving Cert. Maximum 625.
  2. Higher Level: H1 100, H2 88, H3 77, H4 66, H5 56, H6 46, H7 37. Ordinary: O1 56, O2 46, O3 37, O4 28, O5 20, O6 12.
  3. Higher Level Maths: +25 bonus points for H6 or above, so a H6 is worth 71.
  4. LCVP Link Modules can count as a sixth subject: Distinction 66, Merit 46, Pass 28.
  5. Points aren't everything: you must also meet the course's minimum entry requirements (for example a H4 in Maths for engineering).

Example: H3 English, H4 Irish, H5 Maths, O2 French, H4 Biology, H3 Business, H5 Geography = 423 points (best six, including the 25-point maths bonus). Full points table →
