Medicine and the HPAT in 2027

How undergraduate medicine entry works in 2027 – and the big change to how the HPAT counts.

CAO / medical schools 2027 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • You need at least 480 Leaving Cert points, the minimum subject requirements for each medical school, and to sit HPAT-Ireland in 2027.
  • New for 2027: Leaving Cert points above 550 are no longer moderated – your full points count.
  • New for 2027: HPAT is weighted to a maximum of 150 (it was 300). So the top combined score is 775.
  • Because of this, 2026 medicine points can't be compared with 2027 – expect very different numbers.
  • HPAT registration: 5 November 2026 – 15 January 2027 (late until 1 February). Test: 12–15 February 2027. Results: late June 2027.
  • Ties on the combined score are broken by Leaving Cert points.
