Business, accounting and finance courses – CAO points

Business degrees are the biggest group on the CAO list – from general business and commerce to accounting, finance, marketing, HR and supply chain. Many lead to exemptions from professional exams (ACCA, CPA, Chartered Accountants Ireland), so check the official course page if that matters to you.
