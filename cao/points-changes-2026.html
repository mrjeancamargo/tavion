CAO points 2026: what went up and down

Which CAO points went up and down in 2026? The biggest risers and fallers between 2025 and 2026 Round 1.

CAO Round 1 2025 and 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Of 1143 courses with points in both years, 610 went up, 443 went down and 90 stayed the same.
  • Biggest rise: DK732 Construction Management (+180).
  • Biggest fall: SE922 Tourism and Event Management (-245).
  • Points depend on how many people put the course high on their list and how many places there are – a big jump one year often settles the next.
