Key facts

  • LM095 Graphics, Engineering and Technology-concurrent Teacher Education is a Level 8 honours degree at University of Limerick (Castletroy, Limerick, Co. Limerick).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was 421 points, up 22 on 2025 (399).
  • It fell to 420 in Round 2.
  • That's higher than about 66% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 444 points or more (the mid-point).
