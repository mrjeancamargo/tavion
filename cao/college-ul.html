Key facts

  • University of Limerick is a university in Castletroy, Limerick.
  • It offers 62 CAO courses for 2027: 61 at Level 8, 1 at Level 6.
  • 2026 Round 1 points ranged from 56 to 625 (excluding portfolio and HPAT scores).
