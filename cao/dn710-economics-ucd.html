Key facts

  • DN710 Economics is a Level 8 honours degree at University College Dublin (NUI) (Belfield, Dublin 4, Co. Dublin).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was 544 points, up 2 on 2025 (542).
  • It fell to 543* in Round 2.
  • That's higher than about 93% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 544 points or more (the mid-point).
