Munster Technological University: CAO courses and points

Every Munster Technological University course on the CAO for 2027, with 2026 and 2025 Round 1 points and links to each official course page.

CAO 2027 course list · Points 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Munster Technological University is a technological university in Cork and Kerry.
  • It offers 111 CAO courses for 2027: 71 at Level 8, 38 at Level 7, 2 at Level 6.
  • 2026 Round 1 points ranged from 190 to 555 (excluding portfolio and HPAT scores).
  • Highest points: MT871 Biomedical Science (offered jointly MTU Cork Campus and UCC) (555*).
  • Campuses on the CAO list: CCAD Cork, CCAD Envision Centre, CCAD Sharman Crawford Street, Clonakilty/Cork, Cork, Cork School of Music, Cork and UCC, Kerry, NMCI Ringaskiddy.
  • Biggest areas: Art, design, music and film (20), Sport, hospitality and tourism (16), Science and maths (14).
