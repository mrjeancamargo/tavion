Key facts

  • TR006 Psychology is a Level 8 honours degree at Trinity College Dublin (College Green, Dublin 2, Co. Dublin).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was 578 points, the same as 2025.
  • It fell to 577* in Round 2.
  • That's higher than about 97% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
