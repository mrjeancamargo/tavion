South East Technological University: CAO courses and points

Every South East Technological University course on the CAO for 2027, with 2026 and 2025 Round 1 points and links to each official course page.

CAO 2027 course list · Points 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • South East Technological University is a technological university in Waterford, Carlow, Wexford and Kilkenny.
  • It offers 143 CAO courses for 2027: 84 at Level 8, 52 at Level 7, 7 at Level 6.
  • 2026 Round 1 points ranged from 167 to 566 (excluding portfolio and HPAT scores).
  • Highest points: SE526 Veterinary Medicine (work experience required) (#566).
  • Campuses on the CAO list: Carlow, Dublin - Natnl Botanic Gardens, Waterford, Waterford - College Street, Waterford - Granary, Waterford - Kildalton, Waterford or Wexford, Wexford, Wexford - Hill Street.
  • Biggest areas: Business, accounting and finance (19), Sport, hospitality and tourism (19), Computing and IT (18).
