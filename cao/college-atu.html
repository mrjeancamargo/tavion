Every Atlantic Technological University course on the CAO for 2027, with 2026 and 2025 Round 1 points and links to each official course page.

CAO 2027 course list · Points 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Atlantic Technological University is a technological university in Galway, Mayo, Sligo and Donegal.
  • It offers 211 CAO courses for 2027: 138 at Level 8, 62 at Level 7, 11 at Level 6.
  • 2026 Round 1 points ranged from 168 to 544 (excluding portfolio and HPAT scores).
  • Highest points: AU975 Pharmacy (544*).
  • Campuses on the CAO list: Castlebar, Galway, Killybegs, Letterfrack, Letterkenny, Mountbellew, Sligo, Sligo and St Angela's, Sligo, St Angela's.
  • Biggest areas: Medicine, nursing and health (25), Art, design, music and film (24), Architecture and construction (24).
