CAO dates 2027

The key CAO dates for everyone starting college in 2027 – applying, changing your mind, HEAR/DARE and offers.

CAO timetable · Checked November 2026 · Dates may change – always check the CAO

Key facts

  • 5 November 2026 (12:00): CAO applications open for 2027.
  • 20 January 2027 (17:00): apply by now for the discounted €35 fee.
  • 1 February 2027 (17:00): normal closing date. Restricted courses must be on your list by now.
  • 1 March 2027 (17:00): last day to complete the online HEAR/DARE forms and the mature section.
  • 5 May – 1 July 2027: free Change of Mind.
  • August 2027: Leaving Cert results and Round 1 offers (exact dates to be confirmed).

Tips

  • List courses in your genuine order of preference – you're offered the highest one your points reach, so a 'safe' choice high up can cost you a course you'd prefer.
  • Use all 10 Level 8 and 10 Level 7/6 places.
  • Restricted courses (portfolio, audition, some tests) can't be added after 1 February.
  • Apply for a SUSI grant from spring – you don't need an offer first.
