Key facts

  • DK880 Health and Physical Activity is a Level 8 honours degree at Dundalk Institute of Technology (Dundalk, Co. Louth).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was 226 points, down 9 on 2025 (235).
  • It fell to 226 in Round 2.
  • That's higher than about 1% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
