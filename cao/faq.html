CAO points FAQ

Common CAO questions, answered simply.

Checked November 2026

How are CAO points calculated?

Your best six Leaving Cert subjects from one sitting. H1 100 down to H7 37; O1 56 down to O6 12. +25 for Higher Maths at H6 or above. Maximum 625.

What is the Higher Maths bonus?

25 extra points for a H6 or better in Higher Level Maths. A H6 is worth 71 instead of 46.

Does LCVP count for CAO points?

Yes – the Link Modules can count as a sixth subject: Distinction 66, Merit 46, Pass 28. Not every college accepts LCVP for minimum entry requirements.

Are last year's points what I'll need?

No – they're a guide. Points are set by demand and the number of places each August. Use them to judge which courses are realistic, not to rule courses out.

What does * mean on CAO points?

Not everyone on that score got a place – places went to some applicants on that score by random selection.

What does # mean?

The course has a test, interview, portfolio or audition. Scores over 625 include that element.

What does AQA mean?

All qualified applicants – everyone who met the minimum entry requirements and applied got an offer.

How many courses can I list?

Up to 10 Level 8 and 10 Level 7/6 courses, in order of preference. The lists are separate – you can get an offer from each.

What is the difference between Level 6, 7 and 8?

Level 8 is an honours bachelor degree (usually 4 years), Level 7 an ordinary degree (3 years), Level 6 a higher certificate (2 years). Many Level 6/7 courses lead on to Level 8.

When does the CAO close for 2027?

1 February 2027 at 17:00 (discounted €35 fee if you apply by 20 January). Change of Mind is free from 5 May to 1 July 2027.

What is HEAR and DARE?

Access routes giving reduced-points places to students from disadvantaged backgrounds (HEAR) or with a disability (DARE). Apply by 1 February.

Is this the official CAO site?

No – this is a free, independent tool. Apply only at cao.ie.
