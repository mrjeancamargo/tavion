Key facts

  • GC489 Interior Architecture is a Level 8 honours degree at Griffith College (Dublin, Co. Dublin).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was #293 points, down 67 on 2025 (360).
  • It fell to #293 in Round 2.
