How many CAO points is each Leaving Cert grade worth? The official points table, the Higher Maths bonus, LCVP and Foundation Level.

CAO points grid · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Your best six subjects from one sitting of the Leaving Cert count. The maximum is 625.
  • H1 = 100 points, down to H7 = 37. H8 = 0.
  • O1 = 56 – the same as a H5. O6 = 12; O7 and O8 = 0.
  • Higher Level Maths bonus: +25 for H6 or better. A H6 in Higher Maths (71) beats an O1 (56).
  • LCVP Link Modules can replace a sixth subject: Distinction 66, Merit 46, Pass 28.
  • Foundation Level Maths or Irish: F1 = 20, F2 = 12 – but only some colleges award these points.

Is Higher Maths worth the risk?

Almost always, if you can pass it. A H7 in Higher Maths (37) is worth less than an O1 (56), but a H6 is worth 71 because of the bonus. Moving from O1 to H6 adds 15 points in the example above. Many courses (engineering, some science and computing) also require a minimum grade in Higher Maths.

Things that don't add points

  • Repeating subjects in a second sitting – only one sitting counts (but colleges may accept matriculation requirements from different sittings).
  • Transition Year and Junior Cycle results.
  • Leaving Cert Applied – it doesn't qualify you for CAO points (a QQI FET course is a common route on).
  • Extra subjects beyond six – only your best six count.

Portfolios, auditions, interviews and the HPAT are scored separately by some courses – see portfolio and audition courses and medicine and the HPAT.
