Every Trinity College Dublin course on the CAO for 2027, with 2026 and 2025 Round 1 points and links to each official course page.

CAO 2027 course list · Points 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Trinity College Dublin is a university in College Green, Dublin 2.
  • It offers 145 CAO courses for 2027: 142 at Level 8, 3 at Level 7.
  • 2026 Round 1 points ranged from 320 to 625 (excluding portfolio and HPAT scores).
  • Highest points: TR034 Management Science and Information Systems Studies (625).
  • Biggest areas: Arts, humanities and languages (50), Art, design, music and film (17), Medicine, nursing and health (17).
