CAO points for GC403 Law at Griffith College: 2026 cut-off, the trend since 2023 and what you need for 2027 entry.

Key facts

  • GC403 Law is a Level 8 honours degree at Griffith College (Dublin, Co. Dublin).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was 253 points, up 2 on 2025 (251).
  • It fell to 244 in Round 2.
  • That's higher than about 7% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 328 points or more (the mid-point).
