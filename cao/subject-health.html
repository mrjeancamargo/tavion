Medicine, nursing and health courses – CAO points

Medicine, nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy, radiography, dentistry and more. Medicine uses the HPAT test as well as points, and many health courses need Garda vetting. Nursing has a separate quota for mature and QQI FET applicants.

Points 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • 145 medicine, nursing and health courses on the CAO for 2027 (126 at Level 8, 10 at Level 7, 9 at Level 6).
  • 2026 Round 1 points: from 56 to 625; half needed 433 or less.
