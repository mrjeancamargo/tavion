TR009 Music Education (Interview/test) – CAO points

CAO points for TR009 Music Education (Interview/test) at Trinity College Dublin: 2026 cut-off, the trend since 2023 and what you need for 2027 entry.

CAO points 2026 · 2027 course list · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • TR009 Music Education (Interview/test) is a Level 8 honours degree at Trinity College Dublin (College Green, Dublin 2, Co. Dublin).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was #688 points, up 21 on 2025 (667).
  • It fell to #657 in Round 2.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 661 points or more (the mid-point).
  • Scores above 625 mean a test, portfolio or HPAT is added to the Leaving Cert points – they can't be compared directly with your Leaving Cert points.

Entry requirements to know about

  • There's an audition as part of entry.
  • This is a restricted application course: it must be on your CAO list by 1 February 2027 (you can't add it later with a change of mind).
  • Course requires Garda Vetting clearance
