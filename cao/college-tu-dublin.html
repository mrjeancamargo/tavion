CAO 2027 course list · Points 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Technological University Dublin is a technological university in Grangegorman, Tallaght and Blanchardstown.
  • It offers 149 CAO courses for 2027: 97 at Level 8, 45 at Level 7, 7 at Level 6.
  • 2026 Round 1 points ranged from 204 to 566 (excluding portfolio and HPAT scores).
  • Highest points: TU871 Optometry (566).
  • Campuses on the CAO list: Aungier St AND Grangegorman, Aungier Street, Aungier Street AND Bolton St AND Grangegorman, Blanchardstown, Blanchardstown OR Tallaght, Bolton St AND Grangegorman, Bolton Street, Francis Street, Grangegorman, Tallaght.
  • Biggest areas: Science and maths (25), Business, accounting and finance (23), Engineering and manufacturing (22).
