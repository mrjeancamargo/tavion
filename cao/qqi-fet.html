QQI FET points for the CAO

Did a PLC or QQI Level 5/6 course? You can get up to 390 CAO points – and many courses keep places just for you.

CAO QQI FET scoring scheme · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • You need a full QQI FET Level 5 or 6 major award – usually 8 components. A component certificate isn't enough.
  • Each component is weighted by grade: Distinction 3, Merit 2, Pass 1, times its credit value.
  • Add your best components up to 120 credits (distinctions first), then multiply by 13/12. 8 distinctions = 390 points.
  • Some courses need specific components or a minimum number of distinctions.
  • Many courses have a reserved quota for QQI FET applicants, including nursing.
