Key facts

  • CK408 Physics and Astrophysics is a Level 8 honours degree at University College Cork (NUI) (Cork city, Co. Cork).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was 582 points, up 17 on 2025 (565).
  • It fell to 578 in Round 2.
  • That's higher than about 98% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 584 points or more (the mid-point).
