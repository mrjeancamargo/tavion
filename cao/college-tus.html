Every Technological University of the Shannon course on the CAO for 2027, with 2026 and 2025 Round 1 points and links to each official course page.

CAO 2027 course list · Points 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Technological University of the Shannon is a technological university in Limerick, Athlone, Clonmel, Thurles and Ennis.
  • It offers 160 CAO courses for 2027: 103 at Level 8, 51 at Level 7, 6 at Level 6.
  • 2026 Round 1 points ranged from 170 to 478 (excluding portfolio and HPAT scores).
  • Highest points: US925 Applied Psychology (478).
  • Campuses on the CAO list: Athlone, Clonmel, Ennis, Limerick, Shannon, Thurles.
  • Biggest areas: Business, accounting and finance (25), Engineering and manufacturing (21), Art, design, music and film (20).
