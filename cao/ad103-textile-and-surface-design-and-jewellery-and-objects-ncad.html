AD103 Textile & Surface Design and Jewellery & Objects – CAO points

CAO points for AD103 Textile & Surface Design and Jewellery & Objects at NCAD: 2026 cut-off, the trend since 2023 and what you need for 2027 entry.

CAO points 2026 · 2027 course list · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • AD103 Textile & Surface Design and Jewellery & Objects is a Level 8 honours degree at National College of Art and Design (Thomas Street, Dublin 8, Co. Dublin).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was #503 points, down 11 on 2025 (514).
  • That's higher than about 84% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 619 points or more (the mid-point).

Entry requirements to know about

  • You must submit a portfolio – for many art and design courses it's scored and added to (or replaces) your points.
  • This is a restricted application course: it must be on your CAO list by 1 February 2027 (you can't add it later with a change of mind).
  • NCAD Portfolio Submission Deadline: Friday 12th February 2027
  • For information on Portfolio submission guidelines and FAQs, please see https://www.ncad.ie/study-at-ncad/undergrad-portfolio/
