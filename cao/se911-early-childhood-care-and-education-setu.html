Key facts

  • SE911 Early Childhood Care and Education is a Level 8 honours degree at South East Technological University (Waterford - College Street, Co. Waterford).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was 270 points, the same as 2025.
  • That's higher than about 11% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 346 points or more (the mid-point).
