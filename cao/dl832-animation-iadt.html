DL832 Animation – CAO points

CAO points for DL832 Animation at IADT: 2026 cut-off, the trend since 2023 and what you need for 2027 entry.

CAO points 2026 · 2027 course list · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • DL832 Animation is a Level 8 honours degree at Dun Laoghaire Institute of Art, Design and Technology (Dún Laoghaire, Co. Dublin).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was #932* points, down 13 on 2025 (945). Not everyone on that score got a place (random selection).
  • It fell to #914 in Round 2.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 1053 points or more (the mid-point).
  • Scores above 625 mean a test, portfolio or HPAT is added to the Leaving Cert points – they can't be compared directly with your Leaving Cert points.

Entry requirements to know about

  • You must submit a portfolio – for many art and design courses it's scored and added to (or replaces) your points.
  • This is a restricted application course: it must be on your CAO list by 1 February 2027 (you can't add it later with a change of mind).
  • IADT Portfolio submission deadline: 2nd March 2027 @ 5pm
  • For detailed guidelines, see https://iadt.ie/study/portfolio-guidelines/
