MT935 Popular Music - Voice (entrance assessment) – CAO points

CAO points for MT935 Popular Music - Voice (entrance assessment) at MTU: 2026 cut-off, the trend since 2023 and what you need for 2027 entry.

CAO points 2026 · 2027 course list · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • MT935 Popular Music - Voice (entrance assessment) is a Level 8 honours degree at Munster Technological University (Cork School of Music, Co. Cork).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was #448 points, up 17 on 2025 (431).
  • That's higher than about 72% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 457 points or more (the mid-point).

Entry requirements to know about

  • There's an audition as part of entry.
  • This is a restricted application course: it must be on your CAO list by 1 February 2027 (you can't add it later with a change of mind).
  • Course requires Garda Vetting clearance
