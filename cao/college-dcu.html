Every Dublin City University course on the CAO for 2027, with 2026 and 2025 Round 1 points and links to each official course page.

CAO 2027 course list · Points 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Dublin City University is a university in Glasnevin and Drumcondra, Dublin.
  • It offers 71 CAO courses for 2027: 71 at Level 8.
  • 2026 Round 1 points ranged from 342 to 602 (excluding portfolio and HPAT scores).
  • Highest points: DC116 Global Business (USA) (602).
  • Biggest areas: Teaching and education (14), Science and maths (13), Business, accounting and finance (12).
