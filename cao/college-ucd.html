Key facts

  • University College Dublin (NUI) is a university in Belfield, Dublin 4.
  • It offers 40 CAO courses for 2027: 40 at Level 8.
  • 2026 Round 1 points ranged from 61 to 625 (excluding portfolio and HPAT scores).
  • Highest points: DN670 Economics and Finance (625).
