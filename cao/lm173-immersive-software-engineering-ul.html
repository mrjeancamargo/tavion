LM173 Immersive Software Engineering – CAO points

CAO points for LM173 Immersive Software Engineering at UL: 2026 cut-off, the trend since 2023 and what you need for 2027 entry.

CAO points 2026 · 2027 course list · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • LM173 Immersive Software Engineering is a Level 8 honours degree at University of Limerick (Castletroy, Limerick, Co. Limerick).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was #652 points, up 1 on 2025 (651).
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 668 points or more (the mid-point).
  • Scores above 625 mean a test, portfolio or HPAT is added to the Leaving Cert points – they can't be compared directly with your Leaving Cert points.
