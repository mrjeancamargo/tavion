TR052 Dental Science – CAO points

CAO points for TR052 Dental Science at Trinity College Dublin: 2026 cut-off, the trend since 2023 and what you need for 2027 entry.

CAO points 2026 · 2027 course list · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • TR052 Dental Science is a Level 8 honours degree at Trinity College Dublin (College Green, Dublin 2, Co. Dublin).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was 625* points, the same as 2025. Not everyone on that score got a place (random selection).
  • It fell to 625* in Round 2.
  • That's higher than about 100% of Level 8 courses on the CAO list.
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 625 points or more (the mid-point).
