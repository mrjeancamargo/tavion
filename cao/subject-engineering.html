Engineering and manufacturing courses – CAO points

Civil, mechanical, electrical, biomedical and many more. Most engineering degrees need a minimum grade in Higher Level Maths – usually H4 at universities – so check the entry requirements, not just the points. Common-entry engineering lets you pick a branch after first year.

Key facts

  • 125 engineering and manufacturing courses on the CAO for 2027 (77 at Level 8, 47 at Level 7, 1 at Level 6).
  • 2026 Round 1 points: from 165 to 602; half needed 353 or less.
  • 5 courses took all qualified applicants in their latest round.
