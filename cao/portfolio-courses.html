Portfolio, audition and interview courses

103 CAO courses for 2027 use a portfolio, audition or interview. Here's how it affects your points, and the full list.

CAO 2027 course list · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • For many art and design courses the portfolio is scored and added to your Leaving Cert points – that's why some cut-offs are over 625 (up to around 1,000).
  • Some courses only use the portfolio or audition (you must still meet minimum entry requirements).
  • Most of these are restricted: they must be on your CAO list by 1 February 2027.
  • Portfolio deadlines are set by each college – usually in early spring. Check the official course page.
