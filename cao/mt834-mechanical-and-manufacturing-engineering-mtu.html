MT834 Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering – CAO points

CAO points for MT834 Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering at MTU: 2026 cut-off, the trend since 2023 and what you need for 2027 entry.

CAO points 2026 · 2027 course list · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • MT834 Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering is a Level 8 honours degree at Munster Technological University (Kerry, Co. Kerry).
  • In 2026 the Round 1 cut-off was #748 points, up 134 on 2025 (614).
  • Half of those offered a place in 2025 had 894 points or more (the mid-point).
  • Scores above 625 mean a test, portfolio or HPAT is added to the Leaving Cert points – they can't be compared directly with your Leaving Cert points.

Entry requirements to know about

  • You must submit a portfolio – for many art and design courses it's scored and added to (or replaces) your points.
  • Portfolio Submission Deadline: Friday 30th April 2027 Late Portfolio submission: 5 days after the 1st July closing date. For information on Portfolio submission guidelines and FAQs, please see https://www.mtu.ie/courses/mt834/
  • No longer restricted course
