House prices by county

What do homes actually sell for in each county, and what income do you need to buy one? Median sale prices from the CSO for every county.

CSO Residential Property Price Index · 12 months to August 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • National median: €400,000 (+6.7% in a year). First-time buyers: €409,999. New homes: €450,000.
  • Most expensive: Dublin €500,000 (Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown €685,000).
  • Cheapest: Longford €199,000, then Donegal €215,000 and Leitrim €230,000.
  • To buy at the national median, a first-time buyer needs about €90,000 gross income.

Questions

Which county has the most expensive houses?

Dublin – a median of €500,000. Within Dublin, Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown is highest at €685,000.

Which county has the cheapest houses?

Longford – a median of €199,000 in the 12 months to August 2026.

What is the average house price in Ireland?

The national median price was €400,000 in the 12 months to August 2026 (CSO), up 6.7% on a year earlier. First-time buyers paid a median of €409,999.
