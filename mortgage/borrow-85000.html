How much can I borrow on €85,000?

How much can you borrow on a salary of €85,000 in Ireland? Your maximum mortgage, the house price you could afford, the deposit and the monthly repayments.

Central Bank rules · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • On €85,000 a first-time buyer can borrow up to €340,000 (4× income) and buy a home up to about €377,778 with a 10% deposit of €37,778.
  • A second or subsequent buyer (mover) on €85,000 can borrow up to €297,500 (3.5×) – up to about €330,556 with a 10% deposit.
  • Repayments on €340,000: about €1,532 a month over 30 years at 3.53% (the average new mortgage rate, August 2026).
  • Cash you'd need at €377,778: about €46,766 – deposit, stamp duty (€3,778) and fees.
  • That repayment is about 32% of a single person's take-home pay on €85,000 (€4,781 a month).

Buying on €85,000: single or as a couple?

A couple with a combined income of €85,000 has the same limit (€340,000), but more take-home pay, because two people each get tax credits and lower rates. A couple each earning €85,000 could borrow up to €680,000.

Can I borrow more than 4× €85,000?

Possibly. Lenders can approve a share of mortgages above the limits (an "exception"), typically up to about 4.5× income – €382,500 on your salary. Exceptions are limited and go to borrowers with strong finances. Bonus, overtime and commission may be partly counted. Clearing other loans and showing a strong savings record helps.
