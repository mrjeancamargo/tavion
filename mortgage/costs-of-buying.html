The real cost of buying a home

What does it really cost to buy a home in Ireland? Deposit, stamp duty, legal fees and everything else – so you know how much cash you need.

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • You need at least a 10% deposit – €35,000 on a €350,000 home.
  • Stamp duty is 1% up to €1 million, 2% from €1m to €1.5m and 6% above €1.5m. On a new home it's charged on the price excluding VAT.
  • Budget about €5,000 – €6,000 for solicitor, Land Registry, searches, valuation and survey.
  • Total: on a €350,000 second-hand home you'll need roughly €44,000 in cash.

Tips to cut the cash you need

  • Help to Buy (new homes): up to €35,000 back on tax you've paid – it counts towards your deposit. How it works
  • Get 3 quotes from solicitors – fixed fees vary by over €1,000
  • Ask about cashback – some lenders pay 2–3% of the mortgage at drawdown, but compare the rate first
  • Green mortgages – lower rates for homes with a BER of B3 or better
  • Movers: your equity counts towards the deposit – see the movers guide
