€700,000 mortgage repayments

What are the monthly repayments on a €700,000 mortgage in Ireland? See every rate and term, the income you'd need and the total interest.

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Repayments on a €700,000 mortgage are about €3,155 a month over 30 years at 3.53% (the average new rate, August 2026).
  • Over 35 years it's €2,905 a month; over 25 years €3,516.
  • Total interest over 30 years: about €435,817. If rates rose by 2 points, the payment would be about €3,988.
  • To borrow €700,000 you need a gross income of at least €175,000 as a first-time buyer (4×) or €200,000 as a mover (3.5×).
