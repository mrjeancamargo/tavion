How mortgage limits work in Ireland

  1. Income limit (LTI). First-time buyers can borrow up to 4 times their gross income; second and subsequent buyers (movers) up to 3.5 times. Couples add their incomes together.
  2. Deposit (LTV). You need at least a 10% deposit for a home you'll live in (30% for buy-to-let). Help to Buy can count towards it.
  3. Exceptions. Lenders can go above these limits for up to 15% of their home lending – so some people get more, but don't count on it.
  4. Costs on top. Stamp duty (1% up to €1m), solicitor, valuation, survey and Land Registry fees – usually €8,000–€10,000 on a €350,000 home.

Example: a single first-time buyer earning €60,000 with €40,000 saved can borrow up to €240,000 and buy a home of about €272,000 in Dublin, paying about €1,081 a month over 30 years at 3.53% – €1,254 less than the average new rent in Dublin.
