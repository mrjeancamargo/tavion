House prices in Cavan and the salary you need

How much does a house cost in Cavan, and what salary do you need to buy one? Real sale prices from the CSO, with the deposit, stamp duty, mortgage repayments and how it compares with renting.

Key facts

  • The median price paid for a home in County Cavan was €260,000 in the 12 months to August 2026 (CSO) – €140,000 below the national median of €400,000.
  • Prices rose 6.1% on a year earlier (€245,000). Cavan is the 21st most expensive of 26 counties.
  • To buy at the median with a 10% deposit, a first-time buyer needs a gross income of about €58,500 – €29,250 each for a couple (Central Bank 4× limit).
  • Cash needed up front: about €33,810 – €26,000 deposit, €2,600 stamp duty and €5,210 in fees.
  • Repayments on a €234,000 mortgage: about €1,055 a month over 30 years at 3.53% – €149 less than the average new rent of €1,204.
