Help to Buy and the First Home Scheme

Help to Buy and the First Home Scheme can bridge the gap between your mortgage and the price of a new home. Here's how much they give and who qualifies.

Updated for Budget 2027 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Help to Buy: a refund of income tax and DIRT you paid in the last 4 years – up to €35,000 (from 7 October 2026, previously €30,000) or 10% of the price, whichever is lower.
  • Help to Buy is for first-time buyers of new homes or self-builds costing €500,000 or less, with a mortgage of at least 70% of the price. Extended to 31 December 2029.
  • First Home Scheme: the State and participating banks take an equity stake of up to 30% (20% if you also use Help to Buy) in a new home, within local price ceilings.
  • Both count towards closing the gap – but you still need at least a 10% deposit (Help to Buy can make up part of it).

Help to Buy: how much could you get?

  • The lowest of: €35,000, 10% of the purchase price, or the income tax + DIRT you paid in the previous 4 tax years
  • You must be a first-time buyer (all applicants) and live in the home
  • The home must be newly built (or a self-build) and cost no more than €500,000
  • Your mortgage must be at least 70% of the price
  • Apply through Revenue myAccount before you sign the contract

How the First Home Scheme works

You buy the home with your mortgage and deposit; the scheme pays the remaining gap (up to 30% of the price) in exchange for an equal share in the home. You can buy it back at any time. No charge applies for the first 5 years; after that a service charge applies on the equity you haven't bought back. You must borrow the maximum your lender will offer.

Not buying a new home? The Local Authority Home Loan lends to first-time buyers on lower incomes, for new and second-hand homes.

Questions

How much is Help to Buy in 2026?

Up to €35,000 for contracts signed from 7 October 2026 (Budget 2027), up from €30,000 – limited to 10% of the price and the income tax and DIRT you paid in the previous 4 years.

Can I use Help to Buy and the First Home Scheme together?

Yes. If you use both, the First Home Scheme's equity share is capped at 20% instead of 30%.
