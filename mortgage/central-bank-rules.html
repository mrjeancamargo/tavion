Central Bank mortgage rules

The Central Bank of Ireland's mortgage measures limit how much you can borrow and how much deposit you need. Here they are in plain English.

Checked against the Central Bank in November 2026

First-time buyerSecond / subsequent buyerBuy-to-let
Loan-to-income limit4× gross income3.5× gross incomeExempt
Loan-to-value limit90% (10% deposit)90% (10% deposit)70% (30% deposit)
Lender allowance above limits15% of lending15% of lending10% of lending

Exemptions

  • Switching your mortgage to another lender – exempt from both limits
  • Restructuring for arrears – exempt
  • Negative equity buyers – exempt from LTV only
  • Bridging loans for a home (April 2026): exempt from the income limit if 18 months or less with no capital repayments, repaid from the sale. LTV still applies

What lenders also check

  • Repayment capacity – your spending, savings record and other debts
  • Employment – usually out of probation, steady income
  • Credit history on the Central Credit Register
  • Age and term – most lenders want the mortgage repaid by around 68–70
