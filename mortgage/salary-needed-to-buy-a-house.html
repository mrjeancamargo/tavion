Salary needed to buy a house, by county

What income do you need to buy an average home in each county? Ranked from the most affordable to the least.

CSO 12 months to August 2026 · Central Bank rules · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Nationally you need about €90,000 to buy at the median of €400,000 as a first-time buyer – €45,000 each for a couple.
  • Most affordable: Longford – about €44,775.
  • Least affordable: Dublin – about €112,500.
  • Based on the Central Bank limit of 4× income with a 10% deposit. Your bank may lend less (or more, with an exemption).

Questions

What salary do you need to buy a house in Ireland?

About €90,000 gross for a first-time buyer at the national median price of €400,000, with a 10% deposit. It ranges from €44,775 in Longford to €112,500 in Dublin.

How is the salary needed worked out?

First-time buyers can borrow up to 4 times their gross income and need a 10% deposit, so the income needed is 90% of the price divided by 4. Second and later buyers can borrow 3.5 times income with a 20% deposit.
