How much can I borrow? Every income

How much you can borrow for a mortgage in Ireland at every income, under the Central Bank's limits. Couples: use your combined income.

Central Bank rules checked November 2026 · repayments at 3.53% over 30 years

Key facts

  • First-time buyers can borrow up to 4× gross income; second and subsequent buyers up to 3.5×.
  • You need at least a 10% deposit, so the most you can spend is the mortgage plus your deposit (and Help to Buy on a new home).
  • Couples combine incomes: two people on €45,000 each can borrow up to €360,000 as first-time buyers.
  • Lenders can lend above the limits for up to 15% of their loans – so some borrowers get 4.5× income.
