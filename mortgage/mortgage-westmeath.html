House prices in Westmeath and the salary you need

How much does a house cost in Westmeath, and what salary do you need to buy one? Real sale prices from the CSO, with the deposit, stamp duty, mortgage repayments and how it compares with renting.

Key facts

  • The median price paid for a home in County Westmeath was €335,000 in the 12 months to August 2026 (CSO) – €65,000 below the national median of €400,000.
  • Prices rose 8.1% on a year earlier (€310,000). Westmeath is the 12th most expensive of 26 counties.
  • To buy at the median with a 10% deposit, a first-time buyer needs a gross income of about €75,375 – €37,688 each for a couple (Central Bank 4× limit).
  • Cash needed up front: about €42,060 – €33,500 deposit, €3,350 stamp duty and €5,210 in fees.
  • Repayments on a €301,500 mortgage: about €1,359 a month over 30 years at 3.53% – €20 less than the average new rent of €1,379.

Are you a first-time buyer?

You can borrow up to 4 times your gross income with a 10% deposit, and you may get up to €35,000 from Help to Buy on a new home. Help to Buy and First Home Scheme →

Second and later buyers can borrow up to 3.5 times income with a 10% deposit for a home they'll live in. Your current home's equity usually funds the deposit. Moving home →

Buy or rent in Westmeath?

The average new rent in Westmeath is €1,379 a month (RTB, Q1 2026). Repaying a €301,500 mortgage costs about €1,359 a month – €20 less than renting. Buying also means upfront costs, insurance, property tax and repairs. Rent rules in Westmeath →
