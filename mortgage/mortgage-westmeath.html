Buying a home in County Westmeath

Buying a home in County Westmeath? Mortgage, deposit and repayments compared with the average rent, plus Help to Buy and First Home Scheme limits.

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • The average rent for a new tenancy in County Westmeath is €1,379 a month (RTB Rent Index, Q1 2026).
  • Buying a €400,000 home with a 10% deposit means a €360,000 mortgage: about €1,623 a month over 30 years at 3.53% – €244 more than the average rent.
  • To borrow €360,000 a first-time buyer needs a gross income of about €90,000 (single or combined).
  • First Home Scheme price ceiling in County Westmeath: €400,000 for houses.
