First-time buyer guide

A step-by-step guide for first-time buyers in Ireland – from saving the deposit to getting the keys.

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Borrow up to 4× your gross income (combined, if buying with someone) with at least a 10% deposit.
  • New home? Help to Buy gives up to €35,000 and the First Home Scheme up to 30% equity.
  • Lenders want to see 6–12 months of steady saving and rent payments, and few other debts.
  • You'll pay stamp duty (1%) and around €5,000–€6,000 in fees on top of the deposit.

10 steps to buying your first home

  1. Check what you can borrow with the calculator.
  2. Save the deposit – 10%, plus costs. Show regular savings on your statements.
  3. Clear short-term debts and avoid new loans or buy-now-pay-later.
  4. Check your credit report with the Central Credit Register (free).
  5. Get approval in principle from a lender or broker – usually valid 6 months.
  6. Apply for Help to Buy in Revenue myAccount if buying new.
  7. Go sale agreed and pay a booking deposit.
  8. Get a valuation and survey; your solicitor checks the title.
  9. Get mortgage protection and home insurance, then loan offer and contracts.
  10. Drawdown and closing – stamp duty is paid and you get the keys.

Tips from people who've done it

  • Use the Rent Tax Credit while you rent – up to €1,000 a year (€1,150 in 2027). See your take-home pay
  • Keep an eye on your rent increases – they can't be more than 2% a year
  • Joint applicants: both incomes count, and both must be first-time buyers for Help to Buy
  • Gifts from family count towards the deposit, with a gift letter (gifts over the €400,000 Group A threshold may have tax)
