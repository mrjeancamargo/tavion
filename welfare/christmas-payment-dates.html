Social welfare Christmas payment dates 2026

How will social welfare be paid over Christmas and New Year 2026? The Christmas Bonus, payments paid early, the January gap and the first payments at the new 2027 rates.

Checked November 2026 · We'll update this page when the Department confirms the 2026 Christmas schedule

Key facts

  • Week starting 7 December 2026: normal payment plus the 100% Christmas Bonus (a double payment) for long-term payments. Do I qualify?
  • Christmas Day is Friday 25 December, St Stephen's Day Saturday 26 December and New Year's Day Friday 1 January 2027 – all public holidays.
  • In previous years, payments due in Christmas week and New Year week were paid early – often two weeks together just before Christmas.
  • That usually means no payment in the first week of January. Budget for the gap.
  • Budget 2027 increases (+€10 on most payments) start in January 2027.
  • The exact 2026 dates are confirmed by the Department in December – check gov.ie, MyWelfare or your post office.

Which payment do you get?

✅ Yes – you get the 100% Christmas Bonus in the week of 7 December 2026, with your normal payment.

✅ Yes – Disability Allowance, Invalidity Pension, Carer's Allowance (including half-rate) and Carer's Benefit all get the Christmas Bonus.

✅ Yes – One-Parent Family Payment and Jobseeker's Transitional Payment both qualify straight away.

⏳ Only if you've been getting it for 12 months (312 days) or more by the bonus week.

❌ No – Jobseeker's Benefit and Pay-Related Benefit don't get the Christmas Bonus (but you may still get two weeks paid together before Christmas).

❌ No – Child Benefit doesn't get a Christmas double payment. December's payment is on Tuesday 1 December.

Questions

When is the Christmas Bonus 2026 paid?

In the week starting Monday 7 December 2026, on your normal payment day, together with your normal weekly payment.

Are social welfare payments paid early at Christmas?

Usually, yes. In recent years payments due over Christmas and New Year were paid in advance, so many people got two weeks' payment together before Christmas and nothing in the first week of January. The Department confirms the 2026 schedule in December.

Will I get paid in the first week of January 2027?

If your New Year payment is paid early in December (as in previous years), you may get nothing that week – plan your money for the gap. Your first payment at the new Budget 2027 rate (+€10) comes in January.

When is Child Benefit paid in December 2026?

On Tuesday 1 December 2026, then Tuesday 5 January 2027. Child Benefit doesn't get a Christmas double payment.

What if I need help with Christmas costs?

If you're on a low income and not getting the Christmas Bonus, ask your Community Welfare Officer (through your Intreo office) about an Exceptional Needs Payment.
