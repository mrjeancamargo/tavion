Social welfare bonus and lump sum payments 2026–2027

Is there an autumn bonus, a double Child Benefit or a Fuel Allowance lump sum this year? Here's what is – and isn't – being paid in 2026 and 2027.

Not being paid this year

  • Autumn bonus / autumn double payment – not in Budget 2027
  • Double Child Benefit – not in Budget 2027
  • €300 Fuel Allowance lump sum – replaced by a €5 weekly increase
  • General cost-of-living lump sums – replaced by permanent weekly increases (+€10 from January 2027)

Questions

Is there an autumn bonus in 2026?

No. Budget 2027 didn't include an autumn double payment or autumn bonus.

Is there a double Child Benefit payment in 2026?

No. Child Benefit stays at €140 a month with no double payment.

What extra social welfare payments are there this winter?

The 100% Christmas Bonus (week of 7 December 2026) and the €500 Cost of Disability payment (week of 15 February 2027) for people on long-term disability payments.

Is there a cost-of-living lump sum in 2026?

No general cost-of-living lump sum. Budget 2027 put the money into permanent increases instead: +€10 a week on most payments and +€5 on Fuel Allowance from January 2027.

What will you get in 2027?

Your weekly rate after the €10 increase.
