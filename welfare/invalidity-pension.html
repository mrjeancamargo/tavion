Invalidity Pension rates 2026 and 2027

Invalidity Pension rates for 2026 and 2027, who qualifies, how long it's paid and the Christmas Bonus.

Budget 2027 rates · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Invalidity Pension is €269.50 a week from January 2027, up from €259.50 a week in 2026 (+€10).
  • A weekly pension if you can't work because of a long-term illness or disability and have enough PRSI.
  • How long: Paid until 66, when you move to State Pension (Contributory).
  • You get the Christmas Bonus (100% of your weekly payment) in the week starting 7 December 2026.

Who can get Invalidity Pension?

  • Permanently incapable of work, or incapable for at least 12 months and likely to continue for 12 more
  • At least 260 paid PRSI contributions

How long is it paid?

Paid until 66, when you move to State Pension (Contributory).

Tip

Budget 2027 adds a once-off €500 Cost of Disability payment for long-term disability payments.
