Social welfare payment dates

When are social welfare payments paid in Ireland – and what happens around Christmas, the New Year and bank holidays?

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Most weekly payments are paid on the same day every week – the day is shown on your payment notice (it depends on the payment and how you're paid).
  • Child Benefit: first Tuesday of each month. All dates.
  • Christmas Bonus: week starting 7 December 2026, with your normal payment.
  • New rates: Budget 2027 increases start from your first payment in January 2027.
  • Bank holidays (Christmas Day Friday 25 December, St Stephen's Day Saturday 26 December, New Year's Day Friday 1 January 2027): payments due on a bank holiday are usually paid early. The Department publishes the exact Christmas schedule – check gov.ie or your post office.
