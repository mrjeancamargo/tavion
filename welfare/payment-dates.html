Social welfare payment dates 2026–2027

When is social welfare paid in Ireland? Every key date from the Christmas Bonus to the January increases, Fuel Allowance and the €500 disability payment.

Key facts

  • Most weekly payments are paid on the same day every week – the day is shown on your payment notice.
  • Christmas Bonus: week starting 7 December 2026, with your normal payment. Amounts →

Questions about payment dates

When are social welfare payments paid?

Most weekly payments are paid on the same day every week – the day is on your payment notice and depends on the payment and how you're paid.

When is the Christmas Bonus 2026 paid?

In the week starting Monday 7 December 2026, on your normal payment day.

When do the Budget 2027 increases start?

From your first payment in January 2027 – the week of 4 January 2027.

When is the €500 disability payment paid?

In the week starting 15 February 2027, with your Disability Allowance, Blind Pension, Invalidity Pension or Partial Capacity Benefit.

When is Fuel Allowance paid?

Weekly with your main payment from the week of 28 September 2026 to April 2027, or as two lump sums (€532 in September and €602 in January).

Are social welfare payments paid early at Christmas?

Payments due on Christmas Day (Friday 25 December) or St Stephen's Day are usually paid early. The Department confirms the exact Christmas schedule in December – check gov.ie or MyWelfare.
