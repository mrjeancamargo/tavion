Social welfare rates 2027

All social welfare rates for 2026 and 2027 in one table. Budget 2027 increases apply from January 2027.

Department of Social Protection Budget 2027 figures · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Most weekly payments rise by €10 from the first payment in January 2027.
  • State Pension (Contributory) passes €300 for the first time: €309.30.
  • Child Support Payment rises €6 to €64 (under 12) and €84 (12+).
  • Child Benefit stays at €140 a month.
  • Fuel Allowance rises to €43 a week; Living Alone Increase to €25.
