Illness Benefit rates 2026 and 2027

Illness Benefit rates for 2026 and 2027, who qualifies, how long it's paid and the Christmas Bonus.

Budget 2027 rates · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Illness Benefit is €264 a week from January 2027, up from €254 a week in 2026 (+€10).
  • A weekly payment if you can't work because of illness and have enough PRSI contributions.
  • How long: Up to 2 years with 260+ contributions, or 6 months with fewer. Not paid for the first days of illness – check with your employer about sick pay.
  • You get the Christmas Bonus (100% of your weekly payment) in the week starting 7 December 2026 if you've been getting it for 12 months or more.

Who can get Illness Benefit?

  • Under 66 and unable to work because of illness, certified by a doctor
  • Enough PRSI contributions (at least 104 paid)

How long is it paid?

Up to 2 years with 260+ contributions, or 6 months with fewer. Not paid for the first days of illness – check with your employer about sick pay.

Tip

After 12 months you may get the Christmas Bonus. Long-term illness may move to Invalidity Pension.
