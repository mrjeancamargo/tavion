Additional Needs Payment

Big bill you can't cover? The Additional Needs Payment helps with essential once-off costs – and you don't have to be on social welfare.

gov.ie · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • For anyone on social welfare or a low income – working people can apply too.
  • No set amount: a Community Welfare Officer looks at your income, savings and the cost.
  • Apply on MyWelfare.ie or at an Intreo office (form SWA1). Help line: 0818 607080.
  • It replaced the Exceptional Needs Payment.

What it can help with

  • Heating and electricity bills
  • Essential repairs, and replacing appliances or furniture
  • A deposit for private rented accommodation
  • Setting up a home for the first time
  • Funeral costs
  • Regular travel to hospital appointments or to visit relatives in hospital
  • Prams, buggies, cots and child safety equipment
  • Food, clothes and accommodation after an emergency such as a house fire

Questions

What is the Additional Needs Payment?

A once-off payment from the Community Welfare Service for an essential cost you can't pay from your weekly income or savings – such as a big heating bill, a rent deposit or a funeral. It used to be called the Exceptional Needs Payment.

Can I get an Additional Needs Payment if I'm working?

Yes. You don't need to be on social welfare – people working on a low income can qualify, however many hours they work.

How much is the Additional Needs Payment?

There's no set rate. A Community Welfare Officer looks at your household income, savings, expenses and the cost you need help with.

How do I apply?

Online at MyWelfare.ie, or with form SWA1 at any Intreo Centre or Branch Office. Phone 0818 607080 for help.
