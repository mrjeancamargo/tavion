Telephone Support Allowance

An extra €2.50 a week for people who live alone and get Living Alone Increase and Fuel Allowance – paid automatically.

gov.ie · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • €2.50 a week (€130 a year), on top of your main payment.
  • You must get both the Living Alone Increase and Fuel Allowance.
  • Plus one of: State Pension (Contributory or Non-Contributory), Bereaved Partner's Pension, Invalidity Pension, Disability Allowance, Blind Pension, Incapacity Supplement or Deserted Wife's Benefit.
  • No form – it's paid automatically. Missing? Ring Longford (0818 927 770) or Sligo (0818 200 400).

Questions

How much is Telephone Support Allowance?

€2.50 a week – €130 a year – on top of your main payment, Living Alone Increase and Fuel Allowance.

Do I need to apply for Telephone Support Allowance?

No. It's paid automatically if you get a qualifying payment plus both the Living Alone Increase and Fuel Allowance.

I think I qualify but don't get it – what do I do?

Contact the office that pays your main payment: Longford (0818 927 770) for Disability Allowance, Invalidity or Blind Pension; Sligo (0818 200 400) for the State Pension.
