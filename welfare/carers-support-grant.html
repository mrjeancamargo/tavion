Carer's Support Grant rates 2026 and 2027

Carer's Support Grant rates for 2026 and 2027, who qualifies, how long it's paid and the Christmas Bonus.

Budget 2027 rates · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Carer's Support Grant is €2,000 a year from January 2027.
  • An annual grant of €2,000 for carers, paid on the first Thursday of June.
  • How long: Once a year.
2026€2,000 a year
From January 2027€2,000 a year

Who can get Carer's Support Grant?

  • Caring full-time for at least 6 months, or getting Carer's Allowance/Benefit or DCA
  • Not means-tested

How long is it paid?

Once a year.

Tip

Paid automatically if you get Carer's Allowance, Carer's Benefit or Domiciliary Care Allowance.
