Free dental check-up with PRSI

Your PRSI pays for a free dental check-up every year and most of a scale and polish.

gov.ie · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Free oral examination once every calendar year.
  • Scale and polish: the Department pays €50 – your dentist can charge you at most €15 more.
  • Or €42 towards periodontal (gum) treatment if clinically necessary.
  • Fillings, extractions and other work aren't covered – but non-routine dental work gets 20% tax relief.
  • Only dentists on the Department's panel – ask when you book.

Do you qualify?

  • You need PRSI class A, E, P, H or S contributions – most employees (class A) and the self-employed (class S).
  • Under 21: at least 39 paid contributions at any time.
  • Aged 21–28: 39 paid contributions, plus 39 paid or credited in the governing year (2025 for claims in 2027; 2024 for claims in 2026) – or 26 paid in each of the second and third last years.
  • Aged 29–65: at least 260 paid contributions (about 5 years' work), plus the same governing-year test.

How to claim

  • Book with an optician, dentist or hearing aid supplier that's on the Department's panel – ask when you book.
  • Give them your PPS number and date of birth. They check online that you qualify before treatment.
  • Sign the consent form. They claim the money from the Department – you only pay any balance.
  • No application form, no MyWelfare – the provider does it.

Questions

Is the dental check-up free with PRSI?

Yes – one oral examination every calendar year, paid in full by the Department.

How much is a scale and polish with PRSI?

The Department pays €50 and your dentist can charge you at most €15 on top.

What about gum treatment?

If clinically necessary, the Department pays €42 towards periodontal treatment instead of a scale and polish. There's no cap on the balance the dentist charges.

Are fillings covered by PRSI?

No. Fillings and other treatment aren't covered by Treatment Benefit, but non-routine dental work such as crowns can get 20% tax relief.

Do all dentists do PRSI check-ups?

No – only private dentists on the Department's panel. Ask when you book.
