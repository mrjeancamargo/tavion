Back to School Allowance

The Back to School Clothing and Footwear Allowance helps families on social welfare or low pay with school costs.

Key facts

  • €160 for each child aged 2 to 11 (extended to 2- and 3-year-olds in Budget 2026 – check eligibility).
  • €285 for each child aged 12 to 22 in full-time second-level education.
  • Paid automatically to many families in July; others apply from June to 30 September.
  • You must get a qualifying payment (or be on low pay) and meet the income limits.

Getting Working Family Payment, One-Parent Family Payment or Jobseeker's Allowance? You may qualify.
