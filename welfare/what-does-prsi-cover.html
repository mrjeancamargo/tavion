What does PRSI pay for?

Every week you pay PRSI, you're paying for benefits – a pension, sick pay, parent's leave pay, a free eye test. Here's what your class gets you.

gov.ie · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Class A – most employees (pay of €38 a week or more): covers all social insurance benefits and pensions.
  • Class S – self-employed, farmers, some company directors (income €5,000+ a year): covers most benefits but not Illness Benefit.
  • Easy to miss: Treatment Benefit – a free eye test, free dental check-up and up to €1,000 for hearing aids.
  • Check your record any time on MyWelfare.ie.

Questions

What does PRSI pay for in Ireland?

Class A PRSI (most employees) covers every social insurance benefit: State Pension (Contributory), Jobseeker's Benefit, Illness Benefit, Maternity and Paternity Benefit, Treatment Benefit and more.

What benefits do self-employed people get from PRSI?

Class S covers the State Pension (Contributory), Maternity, Paternity, Parent's and Adoptive Benefit, Treatment Benefit, Invalidity Pension, Partial Capacity Benefit, Carer's Benefit, Jobseeker's Benefit (Self-Employed) and others – but not Illness Benefit.

What PRSI class am I?

Most employees earning €38 a week or more are Class A. Self-employed people with income of €5,000 or more a year are Class S. Your class is on your payslip.

How do I check my PRSI contributions?

Log in to MyWelfare.ie with your MyGovID and request a statement of your social insurance record.
