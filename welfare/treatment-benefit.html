Treatment Benefit: free eye test, dental and hearing aids

If you work and pay PRSI, you've already paid for a free eye test, a free dental check-up and up to €1,000 towards hearing aids. Most people never use them.

Who qualifies

  • You need PRSI class A, E, P, H or S contributions – most employees (class A) and the self-employed (class S).
  • Under 21: at least 39 paid contributions at any time.
  • Aged 21–28: 39 paid contributions, plus 39 paid or credited in the governing year (2025 for claims in 2027; 2024 for claims in 2026) – or 26 paid in each of the second and third last years.
  • Aged 29–65: at least 260 paid contributions (about 5 years' work), plus the same governing-year test.
  • 66 and over: 260 paid contributions and enough in the years before you turned 66. Qualify at 60–65 and you keep it for life.
  • Out of work or sick? Credited contributions from Jobseeker's Benefit or Illness Benefit can keep you covered.

Your spouse or partner

A spouse, civil partner or cohabitant who earns €100 a week or less and doesn't get a social welfare payment (Child Benefit, Carer's Benefit and a few others are fine) can claim on your PRSI as a dependant. You both fill in a dependant spouse form, which goes to the Treatment Benefit Section.

How to claim

  • Book with an optician, dentist or hearing aid supplier that's on the Department's panel – ask when you book.
  • Give them your PPS number and date of birth. They check online that you qualify before treatment.
  • Sign the consent form. They claim the money from the Department – you only pay any balance.
  • No application form, no MyWelfare – the provider does it.

Questions

What is Treatment Benefit?

A PRSI benefit that pays for a free eye test, glasses or contact lenses, a free dental check-up, money towards a scale and polish, and up to €500 per hearing aid. Most people who work and pay PRSI qualify.

How do I know if I qualify for Treatment Benefit?

Ask your optician, dentist or hearing aid supplier – they check online with your PPS number and date of birth before you're treated. From age 29 you need 260 paid PRSI contributions plus enough in the governing year.

Is the eye test free with PRSI?

Yes. You get a free eye test once every second calendar year, and money towards glasses or contact lenses. Basic frames are free.

Is the dental check-up free with PRSI?

Yes. One free oral examination every calendar year. The Department pays €50 towards a scale and polish, and the dentist can charge you no more than €15 on top.

How much is the hearing aid grant?

Up to €500 per hearing aid (€1,000 for a pair) once every four calendar years, plus up to €100 towards one repair per aid.

Can my wife or husband use my PRSI?

Yes, if they're your dependant – earning €100 a week or less and not getting most social welfare payments. You both complete a dependant spouse form.

Do I need a medical card for this?

No. Treatment Benefit is based on PRSI, not on income. The medical card is a separate HSE scheme.
