Hearing aid grant: €500 per aid

If you've paid PRSI, the Department pays up to €500 per hearing aid – €1,000 for a pair – every four years.

Key facts

  • Up to €500 towards each hearing aid, once every four calendar years.
  • Plus one repair per aid in four years, up to €100 per aid.
  • Only from suppliers with a contract with the Department – they check and claim for you.
  • Pay more than €500? Claim 20% tax relief on what you paid yourself.

Do you qualify?

  • You need PRSI class A, E, P, H or S contributions – most employees (class A) and the self-employed (class S).
  • Under 21: at least 39 paid contributions at any time.
  • Aged 21–28: 39 paid contributions, plus 39 paid or credited in the governing year (2025 for claims in 2027; 2024 for claims in 2026) – or 26 paid in each of the second and third last years.
  • Aged 29–65: at least 260 paid contributions (about 5 years' work), plus the same governing-year test.
  • 66 and over: 260 paid contributions and enough in the years before you turned 66. Qualify at 60–65 and you keep it for life.
  • Out of work or sick? Credited contributions from Jobseeker's Benefit or Illness Benefit can keep you covered.

How to claim

  • Book with an optician, dentist or hearing aid supplier that's on the Department's panel – ask when you book.
  • Give them your PPS number and date of birth. They check online that you qualify before treatment.
  • Sign the consent form. They claim the money from the Department – you only pay any balance.
  • No application form, no MyWelfare – the provider does it.

Questions

How much is the hearing aid grant in Ireland?

Up to €500 per hearing aid – €1,000 for a pair – once every four calendar years, through Treatment Benefit.

Can I get hearing aid repairs paid for?

Yes – one repair per aid in a four-year period, up to €100 per aid.

Do I need a medical card for a hearing aid grant?

No. Treatment Benefit is based on your PRSI. Medical card holders can get hearing aids through the HSE instead.

Can I claim tax relief on the rest?

Yes. Hearing aids qualify for 20% health expenses tax relief on the amount you pay yourself after the grant.

Where do I get the grant?

From a hearing aid supplier with a contract with the Department. They check you qualify and claim the €500 for you.
