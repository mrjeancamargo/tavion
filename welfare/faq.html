Social welfare FAQ

Common questions about social welfare payments in Ireland.

Checked November 2026

How much is the State Pension in 2027?

€309.30 a week (Contributory) and €298 (Non-Contributory) from January 2027, with higher rates at 80.

How much is Jobseeker's Allowance in 2027?

€264 a week for people aged 25 and over (€173.70 for most under-25s), plus €175.20 for a qualified adult and €64/€84 per child.

When do the Budget 2027 increases start?

From your first payment in January 2027.

When is the Christmas Bonus 2026 paid?

In the week starting 7 December 2026, with your normal payment. It's 100% of your weekly payment.

Is Child Benefit going up in 2027?

No. Child Benefit stays at €140 a month per child.

How much is Carer's Allowance in 2027?

€280 a week under 66 and €318 for carers aged 66 and over, plus the €2,000 Carer's Support Grant.

What is the Cost of Disability payment?

A new once-off €500 payment in 2027 for people on a long-term disability payment.

How much is Fuel Allowance in 2027?

€43 a week during the season – over €1,200 in total.

What is Jobseeker's Pay-Related Benefit?

Since 31 March 2025, people who lose their job get up to 60% of their previous earnings (max €450 a week) for the first 13 weeks, falling to 55% and 50%.

Can I work and get social welfare?

Sometimes. Jobseeker's payments allow work of up to 3 days a week; One-Parent Family Payment and Disability Allowance have earnings disregards (€175 a week from July 2027).

How do I apply?

Most payments can be claimed on MyWelfare.ie with a verified MyGovID, or at your local Intreo Centre.

Is this information official?

No. It's a free, independent guide based on the Department of Social Protection's Budget 2027 figures and Citizens Information. Your entitlement depends on your circumstances.
