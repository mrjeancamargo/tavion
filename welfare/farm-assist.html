Farm Assist rates 2026 and 2027

Farm Assist rates for 2026 and 2027, who qualifies, how long it's paid and the Christmas Bonus.

Budget 2027 rates · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Farm Assist is €264 a week from January 2027, up from €254 a week in 2026 (+€10).
  • A means-tested weekly payment for farmers on low incomes.
  • How long: Paid as long as you qualify.
  • You get the Christmas Bonus (100% of your weekly payment) in the week starting 7 December 2026.
2026€254 a week
From January 2027€264 a week

Increase for a Qualified Adult: €175.20 a week from January 2027.

Who can get Farm Assist?

  • Farming in Ireland
  • Aged 18 to 66
  • Pass a means test

How long is it paid?

Paid as long as you qualify.

Tip

From July 2027 €7,500 of agri-environmental scheme income is disregarded (up from €5,000).
