Child Benefit payment dates 2026 and 2027

Child Benefit is paid on the first Tuesday of every month. Here are all the payment dates for 2026 and 2027.

Checked November 2026

Next Child Benefit payment
Tuesday 1 December 2026
€140 per child

Key facts

  • Child Benefit is €140 a month per child in 2026 and 2027 – no change in Budget 2027.
  • Paid on the first Tuesday of each month into your bank account or at the post office.
  • Twins get 1.5 times the rate per child; triplets and more get double.
  • There's no Christmas double payment of Child Benefit.
