Questions

What benefits am I entitled to in Ireland?

It depends on your PRSI, family, age and income. Most workers can get a free eye test and dental check-up through Treatment Benefit; renters can claim the Rent Tax Credit; families get Child Benefit. Use the checker above to see yours.

What benefits do workers miss most often?

Treatment Benefit (free eye test, dental check and hearing aid grant), the Rent Tax Credit, medical expenses tax relief and Working Family Payment for low-paid parents.

Is this checker official?

No. It's a free, independent guide. Your entitlement depends on your full circumstances – confirm with the Department of Social Protection or Revenue.
