Budget 2027: social welfare changes

What changes for social welfare in Budget 2027 – the new rates, who benefits and when.

Announced 6 October 2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • €10 a week more on most payments from January 2027, including all State Pensions, Jobseeker's, Illness, Disability and Carer's payments.
  • A €500 Cost of Disability payment in 2027 for people on a long-term disability payment.
  • Child Support Payment +€6 per child: €64 and €84.
  • Fuel Allowance €43 a week (+€5) – over €1,200 for the season; better means test for single pensioners.
  • Living Alone Increase €25 (+€3).
  • Working Family Payment thresholds up €30 a week.
  • Christmas Bonus (100%) to almost 1.4 million people in December 2026.
  • No change to Child Benefit (€140).

From July 2027

  • Disability Allowance and Blind Pension earnings disregard up to €175 a week
  • One-Parent Family Payment and Jobseeker's Transitional Payment earnings disregard up to €175
  • Carer's Allowance income disregards up to €1,150 (single) and €2,300 (couple)
  • Increase for a Qualified Adult income threshold from €310 to €340
  • Farm Assist / State Pension (Non-Contributory): agri-environmental disregard €7,500
