Supplementary Welfare Allowance rates 2026 and 2027

Supplementary Welfare Allowance rates for 2026 and 2027, who qualifies, how long it's paid and the Christmas Bonus.

Budget 2027 rates · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Supplementary Welfare Allowance is €262 a week from January 2027.
  • A basic weekly payment if you have no income or not enough to live on, while you wait for another payment.
  • How long: Usually short-term.
  • You get the Christmas Bonus (100% of your weekly payment) in the week starting 7 December 2026 if you've been getting it for 12 months or more.

Who can get Supplementary Welfare Allowance?

  • Living in Ireland
  • No or very low income
  • Pass a means test

How long is it paid?

Usually short-term.

Tip

Exceptional and urgent needs payments are also available through the Community Welfare Service.
