🎄 Christmas Bonus 2026

The Christmas Bonus 2026 is a double payment – 100% of your normal weekly payment – paid in the week starting Monday 7 December 2026. Check if you qualify and how much you'll get.

Citizens Information · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Paid in the week starting 7 December 2026, on the same day as your normal payment – a Thursday payee gets it on Thursday 10 December.
  • It's 100% of your normal weekly payment, including increases for a partner and children. Minimum €20.
  • You don't need to apply – it's paid automatically.
  • Long-term payments only: pensions, Carer's, Disability, One-Parent Family, and Jobseeker's Allowance after 12 months (312 days).
  • Fuel Allowance doesn't qualify. Two qualifying payments = two bonuses.

Who doesn't get it

  • Fuel Allowance on its own
  • Jobseeker's Benefit / Pay-Related Benefit
  • Short-term Illness Benefit (under 12 months)
  • Child Benefit (no Christmas double payment)
  • Maternity, Paternity and Parent's Benefit
