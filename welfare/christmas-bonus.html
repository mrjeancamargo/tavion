🎄 Christmas Bonus 2026

The Christmas Bonus 2026 is a double payment – 100% of your normal weekly payment – paid in the week starting Monday 7 December 2026. Check if you qualify and how much you'll get.

Citizens Information · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Paid in the week starting 7 December 2026, on the same day as your normal payment – a Thursday payee gets it on Thursday 10 December.
  • It's 100% of your normal weekly payment, including increases for a partner and children. Minimum €20.
  • You don't need to apply – it's paid automatically.
  • Long-term payments only: pensions, Carer's, Disability, One-Parent Family, and Jobseeker's Allowance after 12 months (312 days).
  • Fuel Allowance doesn't qualify. Two qualifying payments = two bonuses.

Christmas Bonus: your questions

I moved from One-Parent Family Payment to Jobseeker's Transitional Payment – do I get the Christmas Bonus?

Yes. Jobseeker's Transitional Payment is a qualifying payment in its own right, so there's no 12-month wait. You get 100% of your weekly payment in the week starting 7 December 2026.

I moved from Illness Benefit to Disability Allowance – will I get the Christmas Bonus?

Yes. Disability Allowance qualifies straight away, as long as you're getting it in the week the bonus is paid. (Illness Benefit on its own only qualifies once you've been getting it for 12 months.)

Do you get the Christmas Bonus on Supplementary Welfare Allowance?

Only if you've been getting it for 12 months (364 days) or more. If you don't qualify, ask your Community Welfare Officer about an Exceptional Needs Payment to help with Christmas costs.

I moved from Supplementary Welfare Allowance to Jobseeker's Allowance – am I entitled to the Christmas Bonus?

Jobseeker's Allowance qualifies once you've been getting it for 12 months (312 days). Whether earlier time on another payment can count depends on your claim history – check with your Intreo office.

Does Jobseeker's Benefit or Pay-Related Benefit get a double payment at Christmas?

No. Jobseeker's Benefit and Jobseeker's Pay-Related Benefit don't get the Christmas Bonus. In previous years, though, payments due over Christmas and New Year were paid a week early, so you may get two weeks together before Christmas – that's not a bonus, and it means a gap in early January.

I'm on half-rate Carer's Allowance – how much is my Christmas Bonus?

100% of the half-rate Carer's Allowance you're paid each week, on top of the bonus on your main payment. If your rate is being reviewed, the bonus is based on the rate you're being paid in the week it's issued – if a review later raises your rate, ask the Department whether the difference is paid.

I get two payments – do I get two bonuses?

Yes. If you get two qualifying payments (for example One-Parent Family Payment and Domiciliary Care Allowance, or a half-rate Carer's Allowance with your main payment), you get a bonus for each.

How are payments paid around Christmas and New Year?

The Christmas Bonus is paid with your normal payment in the week starting 7 December 2026. Payments due in Christmas week and New Year week are usually paid early, which often leaves a gap in early January. Christmas payment dates 2026 →

Who doesn't get it

  • Fuel Allowance on its own
  • Jobseeker's Benefit / Pay-Related Benefit
  • Short-term Illness Benefit (under 12 months)
  • Child Benefit (no Christmas double payment)
  • Maternity, Paternity and Parent's Benefit
