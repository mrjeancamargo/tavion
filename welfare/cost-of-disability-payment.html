Cost of Disability Payment rates 2026 and 2027

Cost of Disability Payment rates for 2026 and 2027, who qualifies, how long it's paid and the Christmas Bonus.

Budget 2027 rates · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Cost of Disability Payment: €500, paid once in the week starting 15 February 2027.
  • A new once-off €500 payment for people getting a long-term disability payment, paid in the week starting 15 February 2027 with your normal payment.
  • How long: Once-off lump sum, paid in the week starting 15 February 2027.

Who can get Cost of Disability Payment?

  • Getting Disability Allowance, Blind Pension, Invalidity Pension or Partial Capacity Benefit
  • Paid automatically – you don't need to apply

How long is it paid?

Once-off lump sum, paid in the week starting 15 February 2027.

Tip

It's paid on your usual payment day that week – a Wednesday payee gets it on Wednesday 17 February 2027.

Your questions about Cost of Disability Payment

When is the €500 disability payment paid?

In the week starting 15 February 2027, on your usual payment day, with your qualifying payment.

Do families on Domiciliary Care Allowance get the €500?

No – as announced, it's for adults on long-term disability payments, and Domiciliary Care Allowance isn't on the list. DCA families do get the Christmas Bonus, and DCA rises to €380 a month from January 2027. DCA rates →

Do carers get the €500 Cost of Disability payment?

Not on Carer's Allowance alone – it's paid with Disability Allowance, Blind Pension, Invalidity Pension or Partial Capacity Benefit. Carers get the €2,000 Carer's Support Grant in June instead. Carer's Support Grant →

Do I need to apply for the €500 Cost of Disability payment?

No. It's paid automatically with your Disability Allowance, Blind Pension, Invalidity Pension or Partial Capacity Benefit.
