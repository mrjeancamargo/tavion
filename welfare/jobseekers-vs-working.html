Jobseeker's Allowance vs working

Would you be better off working? Compare Jobseeker's Allowance (€264 a week in 2027) with take-home pay from a job.

Key facts

  • Jobseeker's Allowance is €264 a week from January 2027 (personal rate, aged 25+).
  • A full-time job at the 2027 minimum wage (€14.94 × 39 hours) is €582.66 a week before tax.
  • If you work at least 38 hours a fortnight and have children, you may get Working Family Payment on top of your wages.
  • Use the take-home pay calculator to see your exact net pay.
