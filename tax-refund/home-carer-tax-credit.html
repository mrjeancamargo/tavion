Home Carer Tax Credit

If one of you stays at home to care for children or a relative, you could be owed up to €1,950 a year – back to 2022.

Revenue · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Worth up to €1,950 for 2026 and €2,050 from 2027 (Budget 2027).
  • For married couples or civil partners who are jointly assessed.
  • The person cared for must be a child you get Child Benefit for, someone 65+, or someone permanently incapacitated.
  • Full credit if the carer earns under €7,200 (not counting Carer's Allowance/Benefit); reduced by half of the excess above that.
  • Example: carer earns €9,000 in 2026 → credit of €1,050.
  • You can't claim it and the increased rate band for two earners on the same income – Revenue gives whichever is better.

How to claim

For 2022–2025 (and 2026 after the year ends)
  1. Sign in to myAccount on revenue.ie.
  2. Click Review your tax for the previous 4 years under PAYE Services.
  3. Request a Statement of Liability for the year, then click Complete Income Tax Return.
  4. In Tax Credits & Reliefs, add the credit or relief and submit. Refunds usually arrive within about five working days.
For this year (2026)
  1. Sign in to myAccount.
  2. Click Manage your tax for the current year under PAYE Services.
  3. Choose Add new credits (or Claim tax credits) and select it – your tax credits update straight away.
How much is the Home Carer Tax Credit?

€1,950 for 2026 and €2,050 for 2027. Earlier years: €1,600 (2022), €1,700 (2023), €1,800 (2024), €1,950 (2025).
