Tax refund FAQ

Common tax refund questions, answered simply.

Checked November 2026

How far back can I claim a tax refund in Ireland?

Four years. Right now you can claim 2022–2025 (and 2026 in real time). 2022 closes on 31 December 2026.

How long does a Revenue tax refund take?

Usually about five working days after you submit an Income Tax Return in myAccount.

Do I need to send receipts?

No – keep them for six years in case Revenue asks. The Receipts Tracker in myAccount is the easiest place.

Can I claim the Rent Tax Credit and medical expenses together?

Yes – you can claim every credit and relief you qualify for.

Can I get a refund if I didn't pay tax?

No – credits and reliefs only give back Income Tax you actually paid.

Do I need an accountant or tax agent?

No. Most PAYE claims take about 15 minutes in myAccount. Be wary of agents who take a percentage.

What if I was on emergency tax?

Your employer refunds it once they get a cumulative notification, or you file a return for that year.

Is working from home relief 20%?

No – you can claim 30% of electricity, heating and broadband for the days you worked from home, at your highest tax rate.

Can I claim for my partner's or child's medical bills?

Yes – if you paid them.

Does this checker contact Revenue?

No. It runs on your phone and nothing is sent anywhere.
