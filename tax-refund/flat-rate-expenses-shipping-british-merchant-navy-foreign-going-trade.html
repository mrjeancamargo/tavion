Flat-rate expenses: Shipping - British Merchant Navy Foreign-going trade

Flat-rate expenses for shipping - british merchant navy foreign-going trade: how much you can claim each year and how much tax that gives back.

Revenue flat rate expense list · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Shipping - British Merchant Navy Foreign-going trade can claim a flat-rate expense of up to €318 a year (2026) for the cost of tools, uniforms, fees or similar.
  • That's worth €64 a year at 20% tax, or €127 at 40%.
  • Never claimed? For 2022–2025 you could get back up to €254 (20%) or €509 (40%), plus 2026.
  • You must claim it every year – it isn't automatic.
  • 2022 claims close on 31 December 2026.

How to claim

For 2022–2025 (and 2026 after the year ends)
  1. Sign in to myAccount on revenue.ie.
  2. Click Review your tax for the previous 4 years under PAYE Services.
  3. Request a Statement of Liability for the year, then click Complete Income Tax Return.
  4. In Tax Credits & Reliefs, add the credit or relief and submit. Refunds usually arrive within about five working days.
For this year (2026)
  1. Sign in to myAccount.
  2. Click Manage your tax for the current year under PAYE Services.
  3. Choose Add new credits (or Claim tax credits) and select it – your tax credits update straight away.

Choose 'Your job – Flat Rate Expenses' and pick your job from the list.
