Can I claim tax back on treatment abroad?

✅ Yes

Yes, if the practitioner is registered in that country. Travel and accommodation also count if the treatment isn't available here.

Example: €1,000 spent on treatment abroad = €200 back – or €800 if you claim the same each year for four years.

The rules

  • Relief is 20% of what you paid that wasn't refunded by insurance, the HSE or compensation.
  • The care must be given or advised by a registered practitioner.
  • Keep receipts for six years – you don't send them unless asked.
  • You can claim back four years: 2022 closes on 31 December 2026.

How to claim

For 2022–2025 (and 2026 after the year ends)
  1. Sign in to myAccount on revenue.ie.
  2. Click Review your tax for the previous 4 years under PAYE Services.
  3. Request a Statement of Liability for the year, then click Complete Income Tax Return.
  4. In Tax Credits & Reliefs, add the credit or relief and submit. Refunds usually arrive within about five working days.
For this year (2026)
  1. Sign in to myAccount.
  2. Click Manage your tax for the current year under PAYE Services.
  3. Choose Add new credits (or Claim tax credits) and select it – your tax credits update straight away.
