Budget 2027 tax changes

What Budget 2027 changes for your tax from 1 January 2027.

Budget 2027 (6 October 2026) · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Personal, employee and earned income credits up €125 to €2,125.
  • Standard rate band up €2,500 to €46,500 (single).
  • Rent Tax Credit up to €1,150 (€2,300 for couples) for 2027 and 2028.
  • Home Carer Tax Credit up to €2,050.
  • Rent-a-Room limit up to €16,000.
  • Solar export (microgeneration) exemption up from €400 to €600.
  • USC 2% band extended to €30,300.
  • Help to Buy maximum up to €35,000 (from 7 October 2026).
