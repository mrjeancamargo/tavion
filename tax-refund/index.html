The most-missed refunds

  1. Rent Tax Credit – up to €1,000 a year (€2,000 for a couple), back to 2022. Parents paying a student's rent can claim too. More
  2. Medical expenses – 20% back on GP, consultant, prescriptions, physio, braces, crowns, IVF, even gluten-free food for coeliacs. More
  3. Working from home – 30% of your electricity, heating and broadband for days at home, at your top rate of tax. More
  4. Your job – nurses, teachers, shop staff, builders, hotel staff and 60+ other jobs get a flat-rate expense every year. Find your job
  5. Emergency tax and losing a job – often hundreds back. More

Example: renting at €1,400 a month, €900 a year in medical bills and 100 days working from home could add up to about €5,115 over 2022–2026.
