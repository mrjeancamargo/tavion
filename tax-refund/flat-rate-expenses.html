Flat-rate expenses by job

Over 60 jobs get a flat-rate expense every year for tools, uniforms, registration fees and similar – without receipts. But you have to claim it.

Revenue flat rate expense list 2021–2026 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • A fixed yearly deduction agreed between Revenue and your trade or profession – no receipts needed.
  • Relief is at your highest rate of tax (20% or 40%).
  • Claim it in myAccount for each year – it doesn't happen automatically.
  • Go back four years: 2022 claims close on 31 December 2026.
  • Nurses, teachers, shop assistants, builders, hotel and bar staff, healthcare professionals and many more.

How to claim

For 2022–2025 (and 2026 after the year ends)
  1. Sign in to myAccount on revenue.ie.
  2. Click Review your tax for the previous 4 years under PAYE Services.
  3. Request a Statement of Liability for the year, then click Complete Income Tax Return.
  4. In Tax Credits & Reliefs, add the credit or relief and submit. Refunds usually arrive within about five working days.
For this year (2026)
  1. Sign in to myAccount.
  2. Click Manage your tax for the current year under PAYE Services.
  3. Choose Add new credits (or Claim tax credits) and select it – your tax credits update straight away.

Choose 'Your job – Flat Rate Expenses'.
