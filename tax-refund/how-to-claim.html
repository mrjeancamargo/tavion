How to claim a tax refund in myAccount

Step-by-step: how to claim a tax refund in Revenue's myAccount, for this year and the last four.

Revenue · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • You need a myAccount login (PPS number, date of birth, and a verified email and phone).
  • Past years: request a Statement of Liability, then complete an Income Tax Return and add credits.
  • This year: Manage your tax for the current year – claims update your tax credits straight away.
  • Keep receipts for six years. Use the Receipts Tracker in myAccount.
  • Most refunds arrive in about five working days.

How to claim

For 2022–2025 (and 2026 after the year ends)
  1. Sign in to myAccount on revenue.ie.
  2. Click Review your tax for the previous 4 years under PAYE Services.
  3. Request a Statement of Liability for the year, then click Complete Income Tax Return.
  4. In Tax Credits & Reliefs, add the credit or relief and submit. Refunds usually arrive within about five working days.
For this year (2026)
  1. Sign in to myAccount.
  2. Click Manage your tax for the current year under PAYE Services.
  3. Choose Add new credits (or Claim tax credits) and select it – your tax credits update straight away.

Tips

  • Do one return per year – start with 2022 before 31 December.
  • A Statement of Liability on its own also shows any refund from overpaid tax or emergency tax.
  • Married or civil partners: the assessable spouse usually files the joint return.
  • Never pay a 'tax refund agent' a share of your refund for something you can do yourself in 15 minutes.
