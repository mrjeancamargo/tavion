MyFutureFund vs a workplace pension

MyFutureFund or a workplace pension (or PRSA) with tax relief – which gives you more?

Key facts

  • MyFutureFund contributions get no tax relief. Instead the State adds €1 for every €3 you pay – equal to 25% of the total of €4.
  • Workplace and personal pensions get tax relief at your highest rate – 20% or 40%.
  • For a 20% taxpayer, the State top-up is roughly as good or better. For a 40% taxpayer, tax relief is usually worth more – especially with an employer who contributes more than 1.5%.
  • Compare employer contributions too: many workplace schemes pay more than MyFutureFund's matching rate.
