How MyFutureFund works

  1. You're enrolled automatically if you're 23–60, earn €20,000+ a year and don't pay into a pension through payroll. It started on 1 January 2026.
  2. For every €3 you pay, your employer adds €3 and the State €1 – €7 in your fund. Contributions are on pay up to €80,000.
  3. Rates rise every 3 years: 1.5% now, 3% from 2029, 4.5% from 2032 and 6% from 2035.
  4. You can opt out only in months 7–8 after joining (and after each rate rise). After that you can pause contributions.
  5. You get it at 66: up to 25% tax-free, the rest taxed as income.
