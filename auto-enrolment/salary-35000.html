Auto-enrolment on a €35,000 salary

How much will auto-enrolment cost you on a salary of €35,000? What you pay, what's added, and what you could have at 66.

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • On €35,000 you pay €43.75 a month (€525 a year) into MyFutureFund in 2026.
  • Your employer adds €525 and the State €175 – €700 a year on top of yours.
  • Your take-home pay falls from €29,934 to €29,409 a year.
  • From 2035 you'll pay €175.00 a month and €4,900 a year will go in, in total.
  • Starting at 30, you could have around €434,162 at 66 (about €212,836 in today's money) – an illustration assuming 4% growth, 0.5% charges and 2.5% pay rises.
