Am I auto-enrolled?

Have you been automatically enrolled in MyFutureFund? Here's who is, who isn't and who can choose to join.

Key facts

  • You're auto-enrolled if you're an employee aged 23 to 60, earn €20,000 or more a year across all jobs (or over €5,000 in a 13-week period) and don't pay into a pension through payroll for that job.
  • Around 770,000 workers were enrolled on 1 January 2026 and more than 800,000 by mid-2026.
  • Not auto-enrolled? Employees aged 18 up to 66 can opt in through the MyFutureFund portal.
  • Changing jobs? You stay in MyFutureFund – NAERSA handles it.
  • Start paying into a workplace pension through payroll? MyFutureFund contributions stop automatically.

Not covered

  • Self-employed people (no employer)
  • Employees already in a payroll pension for that job
  • Under 23 or over 60 (unless you opt in)
  • Earning under €20,000 (unless you opt in)
