Auto-enrolment FAQ

Common questions about auto-enrolment.

Checked November 2026

What is MyFutureFund?

Ireland's auto-enrolment pension scheme, started on 1 January 2026 and run by NAERSA. You, your employer and the State all pay in.

How much do I pay?

1.5% of gross pay (up to €80,000) in 2026–2028, 3% from 2029, 4.5% from 2032 and 6% from 2035.

How much does my employer pay?

The same as you. The State adds €1 for every €3 you pay.

Do I get tax relief?

No – instead the State top-up is worth 25% of the total of you plus the State. Contributions come from your pay after tax.

When can I opt out?

In months 7 and 8 after enrolment, and after each rate increase. You get your own contributions back.

What if I opted out?

You'll be re-enrolled automatically after 2 years if you're still eligible.

Can I pause contributions?

Yes, after 6 months, for at least 12 months. Employer and State contributions pause too.

What happens if I change jobs?

You stay in MyFutureFund; NAERSA handles the move.

I have two jobs – what happens?

Eligibility counts earnings across all jobs. You may be enrolled for each job without a payroll pension.

When can I get my money?

At State Pension age (66), or earlier only for ill-health retirement.

Is it worth it?

For most people on standard tax, €7 goes in for every €3 you pay. Higher-rate taxpayers should compare it with a workplace pension.

Is this calculator official?

No – it's a free, independent tool. Projections are illustrations; check your real fund in the MyFutureFund portal.
