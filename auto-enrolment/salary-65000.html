Auto-enrolment on a €65,000 salary

How much will auto-enrolment cost you on a salary of €65,000? What you pay, what's added, and what you could have at 66.

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • On €65,000 you pay €81.25 a month (€975 a year) into MyFutureFund in 2026.
  • Your employer adds €975 and the State €325 – €1,300 a year on top of yours.
  • Your take-home pay falls from €47,563 to €46,588 a year.
  • From 2035 you'll pay €325.00 a month and €9,100 a year will go in, in total.
  • Starting at 30, you could have around €631,032 at 66 (about €309,346 in today's money) – an illustration assuming 4% growth, 0.5% charges and 2.5% pay rises.
