Auto-enrolment at age 35

Auto-enrolment at age 35: how much you could have at 66 on different salaries.

Key facts

  • If you're 35 you have 31 years of contributions before you can take your fund at 66.
  • On €45,000 you could build around €397,061 (€214,907 in today's money).
  • About €99,265 of that could be taken tax-free at 66.
