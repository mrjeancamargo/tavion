Opting out of auto-enrolment

Can you opt out of MyFutureFund, when, and what happens to your money? The rules in plain English.

Citizens Information · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • You must stay in for at least 6 months. You can then opt out during months 7 and 8. Joined on 1 January 2026? Your window was July–August 2026.
  • If you opt out you get back your own contributions. The employer and State money stays in your fund, invested, until you're 66.
  • You're re-enrolled automatically after 2 years if you're still eligible.
  • After month 8 you can't opt out, but you can pause (suspend) contributions at any time – for at least 12 months. Employer and State contributions stop too.
  • After each rate increase (2029, 2032, 2035) there's another opt-out window 6 months later, refunding the extra you paid.
  • You can cancel an opt-out within 48 hours.

How to opt out or pause

  • Log in to the MyFutureFund portal with a verified MyGovID
  • Or contact NAERSA for a paper form (01 568 9555)
  • Your employer can't make you opt out or pause

Before you opt out – think about this

  • For every €3 you pay, €4 more is added by your employer and the State – opting out means turning that down
  • If money is tight, pausing keeps what's already there invested
  • If you're a 40% taxpayer, a workplace pension with tax relief may suit you better – compare
  • If you have a pension through payroll in your job, you weren't auto-enrolled for that job
