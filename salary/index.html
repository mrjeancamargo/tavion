How your take-home pay is worked out

  1. Income tax (PAYE). 20% on income up to your standard rate band (€44,000 for a single person in 2026, €46,500 in 2027) and 40% on the rest. Your tax credits are then taken off the tax.
  2. Universal Social Charge (USC). 0.5%, 2%, 3% and 8% bands on your gross income. You pay no USC if your income is €13,000 or less.
  3. PRSI. 4.2% of your earnings in 2026 (rising to 4.35% from 1 October 2026) if you earn more than €352 a week.
  4. What's left is your take-home (net) pay. We split it into months, weeks, days and hours using your working hours.
