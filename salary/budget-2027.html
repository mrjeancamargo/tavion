Budget 2027: what it means for your take-home pay

What Budget 2027 means for your pay packet: every income tax, USC and PRSI change, and how much better off you'll be from January 2027.

Checked against Budget 2027 tax tables · November 2026

Key facts

  • Budget 2027 was announced on 6 October 2026. The income tax and USC changes apply from 1 January 2027.
  • The 40% tax rate now starts at €46,500 for a single person (up €2,500).
  • Personal, employee and earned income tax credits each rise by €125 to €2,125.
  • The USC 2% band is widened to €30,300 so a full-time minimum wage worker stays out of the 3% band.
  • A single worker on €45,000 takes home about +€398 a year (+€33 a month) in 2027.
  • Employee PRSI is already set to rise to 4.5% from 1 October 2027, which takes back some of the gain.

What hasn't changed

  • Income tax rates stay at 20% and 40%
  • USC rates stay at 0.5%, 2%, 3% and 8% (with an extra 3% for self-employed income over €100,000)
  • You still pay no USC if your income is €13,000 or less
  • Reduced USC for full medical card holders and people aged 70+ earning €60,000 or less – the medical card concession is extended to the end of 2027
  • Single Person Child Carer credit (€1,900), Age credit (€245 / €490), Blind person's credit (€1,950) and Incapacitated child credit (€3,800)

Sources: Department of Finance Budget 2027; Revenue; KPMG Budget 2027 tax tables. Credits not mentioned in Budget 2027 are assumed unchanged.

Budget 2027 questions

When do the Budget 2027 tax changes start?

The income tax and USC changes apply from 1 January 2027. The PRSI rate rises from 4.35% to 4.5% on 1 October 2027.

How much will I get from Budget 2027?

A single PAYE worker on €45,000 takes home about €398 more a year (€33.21 a month). Most full-time workers gain between €200 and €600 a year.

What are the tax bands for 2027 in Ireland?

The 20% rate applies up to €46,500 for a single person, €50,500 for a single parent, €55,500 for a married couple with one income and up to €93,000 for a married couple with two incomes. Income above that is taxed at 40%.

What is the minimum wage in 2027?

€14.94 an hour from 1 January 2027, up from €14.15.
