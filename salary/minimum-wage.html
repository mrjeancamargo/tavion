Minimum wage Ireland 2026 and 2027

Ireland's minimum wage for 2025, 2026 and 2027, and what it comes to after tax per week and month.

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • The national minimum wage is €14.15 an hour in 2026 and €14.94 from 1 January 2027.
  • Full-time (39 hours) on minimum wage in 2027 is €30,298 a year – about €514.11 a week after tax (vs €487.45 in 2026).
  • Under-18s, 18-year-olds and 19-year-olds can be paid 70%, 80% and 90% of the full rate.

Questions

What is the minimum wage in Ireland in 2027?

€14.94 an hour from 1 January 2027, up from €14.15 in 2026.

How much is minimum wage per week after tax in Ireland?

For a 39-hour week, about €487.45 in 2026 and €514.11 in 2027 for a single PAYE employee.
