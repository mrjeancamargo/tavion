Bank holidays in Ireland 2026 and 2027

All the bank holidays (public holidays) in Ireland for 2026 and 2027, and what you're entitled to on each one.

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Ireland has 10 public holidays a year.
  • The next public holidays are listed below – Christmas Day 2026 is on a Friday.
  • St Brigid's Day is the first Monday in February, or 1 February if that's a Friday – in 2027 it's Monday 1 February 2027.
  • When a public holiday falls at the weekend there's no automatic day off on Monday – you get your normal public holiday benefit instead (often a paid day off or an extra day's leave).

Weekend public holidays: St Stephen's Day (Saturday 26 December 2026); Christmas Day (Saturday 25 December 2027); St Stephen's Day (Sunday 26 December 2027). Good Friday is not a public holiday, although many businesses close.

What you're entitled to

For each public holiday your employer gives you one of: a paid day off on the day, a paid day off within a month, an extra day of annual leave, or an extra day's pay. Part-time workers need 40 hours worked in the previous 5 weeks. More on public holiday pay →
