Irish tax rates and credits 2025–2027

Irish income tax bands, tax credits, USC and PRSI rates for 2025, 2026 and 2027 side by side.

Checked against Revenue and Budget 2027 · November 2026 · Download as JSON

Key facts

  • Income tax in Ireland is 20% up to the standard rate band and 40% above it. In 2026 the single person's band is €44,000; in 2027 it's €46,500.
  • A single PAYE employee gets €4,000 of tax credits in 2026 and €4,250 in 2027.
  • USC is charged on gross income at 0.5%, 2%, 3% and 8%. Income of €13,000 or less is exempt.
  • Employee PRSI is 4.2% in 2026, rising to 4.35% on 1 October 2026 and 4.5% on 1 October 2027. No PRSI if you earn €352 a week or less.
