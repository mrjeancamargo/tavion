Annual leave calculator Ireland

Work out how many days of paid annual leave you're entitled to in Ireland – full-time, part-time or zero-hours – and what you're owed for public holidays.

Based on the Organisation of Working Time Act 1997 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Full-time workers get at least 4 working weeks of paid annual leave a year (20 days on a 5-day week).
  • Part-time workers get 8% of the hours they work, up to 4 working weeks.
  • You also get 10 public holidays a year. Part-time workers need to have worked 40 hours in the 5 weeks before.
  • Leave builds up from your first day. You're paid for unused leave when you leave a job.
Your working pattern

How annual leave is calculated in Ireland

The law gives you whichever of these three methods works out best for you:

  1. Working week method: 4 working weeks in a leave year in which you work at least 1,365 hours (unless it's a year you change job).
  2. Calendar month method: one-third of a working week for each month in which you work at least 117 hours.
  3. Hours worked method: 8% of the hours you work in a leave year, up to a maximum of 4 working weeks.

The leave year usually runs from April to March or January to December – check your contract. Hours on annual leave, maternity leave and certain other leave count as hours worked.

Public holiday entitlement

For each public holiday you're entitled to one of these, chosen by your employer:

  • A paid day off on the day
  • A paid day off within a month
  • An extra day of annual leave
  • An extra day's pay

Full-time workers qualify straight away. Part-time workers qualify if they've worked at least 40 hours in the 5 weeks before the public holiday. If a public holiday falls on a day you don't normally work, you get one-fifth of your weekly pay.

Annual leave questions

How many days annual leave am I entitled to in Ireland?

At least 4 working weeks a year if you work full-time – 20 days on a 5-day week. Part-time workers get 8% of the hours they work, up to 4 working weeks. Your contract can give you more.

Do part-time workers get annual leave in Ireland?

Yes. Part-time workers get 8% of the hours they work as paid leave, up to 4 working weeks. For example, 1,000 hours worked gives 80 hours of leave.

Am I paid for public holidays if I'm part-time?

Yes, if you've worked at least 40 hours in the 5 weeks before the public holiday. If it's a day you don't normally work, you get one-fifth of your weekly pay.

Do I get paid for unused annual leave when I leave a job?

Yes. When you leave, your employer must pay you for any annual leave you've built up and not taken, and for a public holiday that falls in your last week if you worked 4 weeks before it.
