How it works

Mock exam: 40 random questions with a 45-minute timer, like the real test. You can move back and forth, flag questions to come back to, and see your score at the end.

Practise by topic: answer questions one topic at a time and see the correct answer and explanation straight away.

Your progress is only kept in this browser tab while you take the test. Close the tab and it's gone — nothing is saved about you.

Practice questions written to help you learn the Rules of the Road. This site is independent and is not affiliated with the Road Safety Authority (RSA) or the official Driver Theory Test. Always check the current Rules of the Road.
