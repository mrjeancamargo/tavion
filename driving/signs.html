Irish road signs and their meanings

Learn the signs you'll see in the theory test. Turn on quiz mode to hide the meanings and test yourself, then practise real sign questions.

Regulatory signs

Red circles and the STOP and YIELD signs give orders. A red slash means something is prohibited.

Speed limit signs

A number in a red ring is the maximum speed in km/h. It's a limit, not a target.

Mandatory signs

Blue circles tell you what you must do.

Warning signs

Yellow diamonds with black symbols warn you about hazards ahead.
