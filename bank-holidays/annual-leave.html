Annual leave entitlement

How much annual leave you're entitled to in Ireland – full-time, part-time or zero-hours – with a calculator.

Citizens Information · Checked November 2026

Annual leave calculator
–

Key facts

  • Most employees get 4 working weeks a year (20 days for a 5-day week).
  • Three ways to calculate – you get whichever is best for you: 4 weeks if you worked 1,365+ hours; a third of a week for each month with 117+ hours; or 8% of hours worked (max 4 weeks).
  • Part-time workers usually use the 8% method.
  • Leave keeps building up while on certified sick leave, maternity, paternity, parental and adoptive leave.
  • Sick while on holiday? Get a medical cert and those days don't count as leave.
  • Your employer decides when you take leave, but must consult you at least a month before. Leave can be carried over up to 6 months by agreement (15 months if you couldn't take it due to illness).
  • Leaving your job? You must be paid for leave you haven't taken.
  • The legal leave year runs 1 April – 31 March, though many employers use January–December.
