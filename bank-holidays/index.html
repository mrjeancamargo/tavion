2027 vs 2026

Key facts

  • 2027 has 8 bank holidays on weekdays, against 9 in 2026 – because Christmas Day and St Stephen's Day 2027 fall on a Saturday and Sunday.
  • You're still entitled to the public holiday benefit for those days (a day's pay, an extra day of leave, or a day off within a month) – but not automatically the Monday off. How it works
  • 2027 has 7 long weekends (holiday on a Monday or Friday) against 7 in 2026.
  • Easter Monday 2027 is early: 29 March 2027.
