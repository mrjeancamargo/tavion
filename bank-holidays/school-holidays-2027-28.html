School holidays 2027/28

The official school holiday dates for 2027/28 for all primary and post-primary schools in Ireland.

Department of Education Circular 0018/2026 · Checked November 2026

BreakDates (schools closed)
October mid-term25 October 2027 – 29 October 2027
Christmas23 December 2027 – 5 January 2028
February mid-term21 February 2028 – 25 February 2028
Easter10 April 2028 – 21 April 2028

Key facts

  • February mid-term: post-primary schools close for the full week; primary schools must close Thursday and Friday and may use discretionary days to close the full week.
  • Christmas: the last school day is Wednesday 22 December 2027.
  • Easter: the last day of term is Friday 7 April 2028; schools reopen Monday 24 April 2028.
  • Schools may shorten the February and Easter breaks to make up for unplanned closures (for example, storms).
  • Start and end of the school year vary by school – check your school's calendar.
