Is Good Friday a bank holiday?

No – Good Friday is not a public holiday in Ireland, even though many schools and businesses close.

Key facts

  • You have no automatic right to the day off or extra pay on Good Friday.
  • Many employers give it off anyway – check your contract.
  • Schools are normally already on Easter holidays.
  • Easter Monday is a public holiday.
