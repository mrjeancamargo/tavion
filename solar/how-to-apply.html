How to apply for the solar panel grant

How to get the SEAI solar panel grant, in the right order. Get one step wrong – like starting work before your offer – and you lose the grant.

SEAI · Checked November 2026

  1. Get 3 quotes from SEAI-registered solar PV companies. Only registered companies qualify. Ask how much the system will make a year, the total price, warranties and whether your roof suits solar. What to ask →
  2. Apply online to SEAI (it takes about 10 minutes). You need your MPRN (on your electricity bill), the company you picked and the system size. Your Letter of Offer comes by email.
  3. Check the offer before work starts. The grant is only paid for the size, company and amount in the letter. To change anything, email SEAI before works begin.
  4. Your installer tells ESB Networks (the NC6 form). This takes at least 4 weeks (20 working days), so it's done before the install.
  5. The installation. Usually 1–2 days. Keep the safety file your installer gives you.
  6. Book a post-works BER. Every applicant needs one, done by an SEAI-registered BER assessor. Book it as soon as you have an install date.
  7. Your installer uploads the paperwork and SEAI pays the grant – usually within 4–6 weeks. The claim, in detail →
The 3 mistakes that cost people the grant: starting work before the offer, the BER not being published, and going over the 8 months.

What you need to apply

  • Your MPRN – the 11-digit number on your electricity bill
  • Your PPS number and contact details
  • The name of your SEAI-registered solar company
  • The system size (kWp) and whether you're adding a battery
  • Your bank details (yours or the solar company's) for the payment

Panels and battery together

Doing both? Apply once – there's no need for two applications. Already applied for panels? Make a new application for the battery; you don't change the first one. You can't apply for anything already installed.

Owner management companies (apartments)

The OMC or its property management agent applies, with written authorisation from the OMC. Contact SEAI (solarpv@seai.ie or 01 808 2100) for the forms.
