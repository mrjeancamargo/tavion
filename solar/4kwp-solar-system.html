4 kWp solar system

How much does a 4 kWp solar system produce in Ireland, what does it cost and how long does it take to pay back?

Key facts

  • A 4 kWp system is about 10 panels of 440 W, needing roughly 20 m² of roof.
  • It makes about 3,388–4,324 kWh a year depending on your county (3,928 kWh in Dublin).
  • Typical price €7,000, SEAI grant €1,800 – you pay about €5,200.
  • In Dublin with a €250 bill it pays back in about 5.4 years (7 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Best for a typical 3-bed home using 3,500–4,500 kWh – the most popular size.
