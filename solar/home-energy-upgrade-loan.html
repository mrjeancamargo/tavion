Home Energy Upgrade Loan for solar

You can borrow for solar panels at a low rate under the Government-backed Home Energy Upgrade Loan Scheme.

SBCI scheme · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Loans of €5,000 to €75,000 for home energy upgrades that also get an SEAI grant – solar PV included.
  • Rates from about 3% to 4.15% depending on the lender and the amount (e.g. PTSB from January 2026: 4.15% under €15,000, down to 2.99% for €50,000+).
  • Lenders include AIB, Bank of Ireland, PTSB and some credit unions.
  • The scheme is open until 31 December 2026 or until it's fully used – check with your lender.
  • Example: €5,200 over 7 years at 3.5% is about €70 a month, against about €79 a month saved in Dublin.

Note: the minimum loan is €5,000, so it suits solar with a battery or bigger upgrades. For smaller amounts, compare a credit union or green personal loan.
