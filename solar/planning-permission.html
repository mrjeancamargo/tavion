Planning permission for solar panels

Do you need planning permission for solar panels in Ireland? For most homes, no.

gov.ie · S.I. 493/2022 · November 2026

Key facts

  • Since October 2022, rooftop solar on houses is exempt from planning permission with no limit on the roof area – including in Solar Safeguarding Zones near airports.
  • There are conditions on how the panels sit – for example, keeping a gap from the edge of the roof and not sticking up too far from the roof. A registered installer will design to these.
  • Free-standing panels in the garden are exempt up to 25 m², with conditions (e.g. not in front of the house).
  • You do need permission for a protected structure, and usually in an Architectural Conservation Area – check with your council.
  • Apartments, businesses, farms and other buildings have their own exemptions and limits.

Check before you start

  • Is your house a protected structure? Your council's Record of Protected Structures will say.
  • Are you in an Architectural Conservation Area? Ask the council's planning department.
  • In an apartment or managed estate? You'll need the owner management company's permission for a shared roof.
  • Want panels on a shed or ground mount? Check the 25 m² and placement rules.

The rules are in the Exempted Development (No. 3) Regulations 2022. If in doubt, ask your council for a Section 5 declaration.
