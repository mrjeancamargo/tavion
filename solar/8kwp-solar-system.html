8 kWp solar system

How much does a 8 kWp solar system produce in Ireland, what does it cost and how long does it take to pay back?

Key facts

  • A 8 kWp system is about 19 panels of 440 W, needing roughly 40 m² of roof.
  • It makes about 6,776–8,648 kWh a year depending on your county (7,856 kWh in Dublin).
  • Typical price €11,600, SEAI grant €1,800 – you pay about €9,800.
  • In Dublin with a €250 bill it pays back in about 5.7 years (6.6 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Best for a large home with an EV, heat pump and battery (may need a three-phase supply or an NC7 application).
