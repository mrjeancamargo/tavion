Solar panels: questions answered

Your solar panel questions answered.

Checked November 2026

How much is the SEAI solar grant in 2026?

€700 per kWp for the first 2 kWp, then €200 per kWp up to 4 kWp – up to €1,800. Plus €600 for a battery of 5 kWh or more.

Does my house qualify for the solar grant?

If it has an MPRN, was built and occupied before 2025, and hasn't had a solar PV grant before. Homeowners and private landlords can apply.

Can I start the work and apply later?

No. You must have your SEAI grant offer before any work starts, or you won't get the grant.

How long do I have to finish the install?

8 months from your grant offer, including the post-works BER and paperwork.

How long does the grant take to be paid?

Usually 4–6 weeks after your company uploads the documents and your BER is published.

How long do solar panels take to pay back?

About 5.4 years for a typical 4 kWp system in Dublin with the grant – roughly 5 to 8 years across Ireland.

How many solar panels do I need?

Match the system to your yearly use: kWh ÷ about 930 = kWp. A typical home using 3,700 kWh suits about 4 kWp – 9 or 10 panels.

Do solar panels work in Ireland's weather?

Yes. Ireland averages about 935 kWh per kWp a year. Panels work from daylight, including cloudy days, though winter output is much lower.

Is a battery worth it?

It helps you use more of your own power. With the €600 grant it can pay off, especially if you're out during the day or on a night-rate tariff. Try both in the calculator.

Will I be paid for the power I export?

Yes, about 15–19.5c per kWh under the Clean Export Guarantee, credited on your bill. You need a smart meter and an NC6 form.

Is solar export income taxed?

Not up to €400 a year (2024–2028), rising to €600 from 2027.

Is there VAT on solar panels?

No – 0% VAT on supply and installation on homes since May 2023.

Do I need planning permission?

Not for rooftop panels on most houses. Protected structures and many Architectural Conservation Areas need permission.

Do solar panels improve my BER?

Yes – you need a post-works BER for the grant anyway, and solar usually improves the rating.

Can renters get solar panels?

Renters can't apply, but landlords can, and a Dublin pilot gives up to €9,000 for RAS tenancies.

What if I move house?

The panels stay with the house and usually add to its appeal and BER. Payback is shorter than most people stay in a home.

How long do solar panels last?

25–30 years, losing about 0.5% output a year. The inverter usually needs replacing once, around year 10–15.

Do I need to clean solar panels?

Rarely in Ireland – rain does most of it. Check now and then for leaves or bird mess.

Can I add a battery later?

Yes, and you can claim the €600 battery grant for it as long as you apply before it's installed.

Is this calculator official?

No – it's a free, independent tool using PVGIS data and SEAI's published grant rules. Always get quotes from SEAI-registered companies.
