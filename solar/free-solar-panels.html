Free solar panels and higher grants

Some households can get solar panels and a battery fully paid, or a grant of up to €9,000. Here's who qualifies.

SEAI · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Enhanced solar grant: €3,350 per kWp for the first 2 kWp, then €850 per kWp up to 4 kWp – up to €8,400, or €9,000 with a 5 kWh battery.
  • For homeowners who live in the home and get a qualifying Department of Social Protection payment. SEAI checks this with the DSP directly.
  • Warmer Homes Scheme: solar up to 4 kWp and a 5 kWh battery are now offered as standard, fully funded.
  • Medically vulnerable: the fully-funded scheme now covers up to 4 kWp plus a 5 kWh battery.
  • On the enhanced grant a 4 kWp system in Dublin costs you about €0 and pays back in about 0 years.

Qualifying payments

  • Fuel Allowance (National Fuel Scheme)
  • Working Family Payment
  • One-Parent Family Payment
  • Domiciliary Care Allowance
  • Jobseeker's Allowance – for over 6 months with a child under 7 (send the child's birth certificate)
  • Disability Allowance – for over 6 months with a child under 7 (send the child's birth certificate)
  • Carer's Allowance – living with the person you care for (send the Carer's Allowance Confirmation Form)

In most cases the homeowner must get the payment – the exception is Carer's Allowance. If your payment only just started, wait 5–6 weeks after the first one before applying.

Renters

Renters can't apply themselves, but landlords can – private landlords qualify for the standard grant. In Dublin, landlords with a long-term tenant on the Rental Accommodation Scheme (Dublin City, DLR, Fingal or South Dublin councils) can get up to €9,000 in a pilot – contact the council first for a letter confirming it.
