Solar panels in Leitrim

Solar panels in Leitrim: how much they make, what you'd save and how long they take to pay back – with the SEAI grant.

PVGIS (EU JRC) · SEAI grant · November 2026

Key facts

  • Leitrim gets about 880 kWh per kWp a year on a south-facing roof – 24th of 26 counties, 6% below the Irish average.
  • A 4 kWp system makes about 3,520 kWh a year and costs about €5,200 after the €1,800 grant.
  • With a €250 bill every 2 months it saves about €861 in year 1 and pays back in 6 years (7.6 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Over 25 years that's about €16,602 ahead.
  • Best month: May (476 kWh). Worst: December (92 kWh).
