What size solar system do I need?

What size solar system do you need? Match it to the electricity you use in a year.

Key facts

  • Rule of thumb: yearly kWh ÷ {AVG} = kWp. A home using 3,800 kWh suits about 4 kWp.
  • Your yearly use is on your bills – or roughly your 2-monthly bill × 6, less the standing charge, divided by the unit rate.
  • The grant maxes out at 4 kWp, but going bigger can still pay if you have an EV or heat pump.
  • Most single-phase homes can connect up to about 6 kW with an NC6 form.
