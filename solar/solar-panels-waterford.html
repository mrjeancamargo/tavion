Solar panels in Waterford

Solar panels in Waterford: how much they make, what you'd save and how long they take to pay back – with the SEAI grant.

PVGIS (EU JRC) · SEAI grant · November 2026

Key facts

  • Waterford gets about 1047 kWh per kWp a year on a south-facing roof – 2nd of 26 counties, 12% above the Irish average.
  • A 4 kWp system makes about 4,188 kWh a year and costs about €5,200 after the €1,800 grant.
  • With a €250 bill every 2 months it saves about €1,000 in year 1 and pays back in 5.2 years (6.7 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Over 25 years that's about €20,060 ahead.
  • Best month: May (532 kWh). Worst: December (132 kWh).
