SEAI solar panel grant 2026

How much is the SEAI solar panel grant, who gets it, and what changed in October 2026.

SEAI · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • €700 per kWp for the first 2 kWp, then €200 per kWp up to 4 kWp. Maximum €1,800.
  • New: a flat €600 battery grant for a battery of 5 kWh or more – up to €2,400 with panels.
  • Who: homeowners, private landlords, owner management companies and approved housing bodies. The home needs an MPRN and must be built and occupied before 2025 (it was before 2021 until 6 October 2026).
  • One grant per home: you can't get it if solar PV was already grant-funded at that MPRN.
  • Apply first: you must have your grant offer before any work starts. You then have 8 months to finish and submit the paperwork.
  • The grant is paid pro rata – e.g. €1,500 for 2.5 kWp. You get the rate in force on the day you apply.

The grant stops growing at 4 kWp, but bigger systems can still be worth it if you use a lot of power (EV, heat pump).

Higher grants

  • Qualifying welfare payment (Fuel Allowance, Working Family Payment, One-Parent Family Payment, Domiciliary Care Allowance and some others): up to €8,400 for panels and €9,000 with a battery. Who qualifies →
  • Warmer Homes Scheme and medically vulnerable households: solar up to 4 kWp plus a 5 kWh battery, fully funded.
  • Dublin RAS tenancies (pilot): landlords can get up to €9,000 for solar and battery.

What changed in 2026

  • The grant stayed at €1,800 for 2026 – a planned cut to €1,500 didn't go ahead.
  • From 6 October 2026: homes built before 2025 qualify (was before 2021), the €600 battery grant started, and fully-funded solar + battery was added for Warmer Homes.
  • Grant levels for 2027 haven't been announced as a cut – but SEAI can change rates, and you get the amount in force when you apply. An approved offer locks it in for 8 months.

Individual grant or One Stop Shop?

Doing just solar? Apply yourself (the individual grant) – it's simpler. Doing a full retrofit to a B2 BER (insulation, heat pump and solar)? A One Stop Shop takes the grants off the price up front and manages it all.
