Solar panels in Ireland: the short version

  1. A typical home with a €250 bill every 2 months suits a 4 kWp system (10 panels). In Dublin it makes about 3,928 kWh a year and pays back in about 5.4 years.
  2. The SEAI grant is €700 per kWp for the first 2 kWp and €200 per kWp up to 4 kWp – €1,800 max. Since October 2026 there's also €600 for a battery of 5 kWh or more. Grant details →
  3. Apply before any work starts. Then you have 8 months to install, get a BER and submit the paperwork. Step by step →
  4. You're paid for what you don't use – about 15–19.5c per kWh under the Clean Export Guarantee, and up to €400 a year is tax-free (€600 from 2027). Export rates →
  5. A battery lets you use more of your own power, but with the €600 grant a 5 kWh battery takes this example from 5.4 years to 7 years. Is a battery worth it? →
