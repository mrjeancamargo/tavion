Claiming your solar grant payment

Panels are on the roof – now how do you get your money? What SEAI needs, who sends it and how long it takes.

SEAI · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • You don't fill in a claim form yourself: your solar company uploads the documents to SEAI.
  • Your post-works BER must be published on the National BER Register before SEAI pays.
  • SEAI pays into your bank account (or the company's) usually 4–6 weeks after it has everything.
  • It all has to be done within 8 months of your grant offer, or the offer expires.

The documents SEAI needs

  • Declaration of Works – signed by the installer
  • Inspection, Test and Commissioning Certificate
  • Safe Electric (RECI) certificate
  • ESB Networks NC6 form
  • Post-works BER – published by your BER assessor
  • Invoice describing the work
  • Photos of the installation (listed on the Declaration of Works)

Why grant payments get delayed

  • The BER isn't published yet. Book the assessor for right after the install and ask them to publish it straight away.
  • Missing documents. Ask your company to upload everything within 1–3 days of the install, and check in your SEAI portal.
  • Wrong bank details. Check them in the portal – they must be yours (the applicant) or the solar company's.
  • The job doesn't match the offer – a bigger system or a different company, without SEAI's approval first.
  • An inspection. SEAI inspects some homes. If rework is needed, payment follows about 2 weeks after it's done.

Check where your claim is

Log in to the SEAI online portal to see the status of your request for payment. Questions: 01 808 2100 or solarpv@seai.ie. Technical questions: 01 277 6977.

Keep these for later

Keep all invoices, receipts and certificates. SEAI can inspect your home in future, and you'll need them for your BER and when you sell the house.
