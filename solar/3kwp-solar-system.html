3 kWp solar system

How much does a 3 kWp solar system produce in Ireland, what does it cost and how long does it take to pay back?

Key facts

  • A 3 kWp system is about 7 panels of 440 W, needing roughly 15 m² of roof.
  • It makes about 2,541–3,243 kWh a year depending on your county (2,946 kWh in Dublin).
  • Typical price €5,850, SEAI grant €1,600 – you pay about €4,250.
  • In Dublin with a €250 bill it pays back in about 5.7 years (7.5 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Best for a smaller home using 2,500–3,500 kWh.
