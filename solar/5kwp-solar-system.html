5 kWp solar system

How much does a 5 kWp solar system produce in Ireland, what does it cost and how long does it take to pay back?

Key facts

  • A 5 kWp system is about 12 panels of 440 W, needing roughly 25 m² of roof.
  • It makes about 4,235–5,405 kWh a year depending on your county (4,910 kWh in Dublin).
  • Typical price €8,150, SEAI grant €1,800 – you pay about €6,350.
  • In Dublin with a €250 bill it pays back in about 5.5 years (6.8 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Best for a bigger home, or one with a heat pump.
