Do solar panels work in winter?

Do solar panels work in an Irish winter? Yes, but much less. Here's what to expect month by month.

PVGIS (EU JRC) · November 2026

4 kWp in Dublin: kWh each month

Dashed line: a typical home uses about 300 kWh a month.

A typical home uses about 300 kWh a month.

Key facts

  • In Dublin, 4 kWp makes about 136 kWh in December but 500 kWh in May – nearly 4 times more.
  • April to September makes about 67% of the year's power.
  • Panels work on cloudy days – from daylight, not heat. Cold weather actually helps them run efficiently.
  • In winter you'll still buy most of your power, so payback is based on the whole year, not just summer.

Getting more from solar in winter

  • A battery on a night-rate tariff can charge cheaply at night in winter
  • Snow slides off tilted panels quickly; you don't need to clean them
  • A hot-water diverter is less useful in winter – there's little surplus
