Choosing a solar installer

Picking the right solar company matters more than picking the right panel. What to ask, and the red flags.

Key facts

  • Only SEAI-registered solar PV companies qualify for the grant – check the SEAI register before you sign.
  • Get at least 3 quotes and compare the price per kWp and the expected kWh a year.
  • Get a written contract with the works, price, timing, payment terms and warranties.
  • Talk to previous customers and check reviews.

Questions to ask every installer

  • How many kWh will it make a year – and how did you work that out?
  • What's the total price, including scaffolding, VAT (0%) and any fuse board work?
  • Which panels and inverter, and what are the warranties? (Panels typically 25 years on output; inverters 5–12)
  • Will you submit the NC6 to ESB Networks and upload all the SEAI paperwork?
  • Is my roof and electricity use suitable? Any shading?
  • What ongoing service or maintenance is needed, and what does it cost?
  • Is the battery or inverter backup-capable, if I want that?

Red flags

  • Pressure to sign today or a 'today only' discount
  • Asking you to start before your SEAI grant offer arrives
  • A big deposit up front (more than about 25%)
  • Payback claims far shorter than our calculator shows
  • Not registered with SEAI, or vague about who does the install
  • No written quote with a model number for the panels and inverter

SEAI doesn't approve or guarantee any company's work – your contract is with the company.
