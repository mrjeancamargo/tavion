Are solar panels worth it in Ireland?

Are solar panels worth it in Ireland? For most homes, yes – here's the honest maths, including when they aren't.

PVGIS data · SEAI grant · November 2026

Key facts

  • A typical 4 kWp system pays back in 5 years (sunniest county) to 6.2 years (least sunny).
  • Panels last 25–30 years, so you could be €18,727 ahead over 25 years in Dublin (with the inverter replaced once).
  • It's worth it most when you use power during the day, have a south, south-east or south-west roof and pay a high unit rate.
  • It's less worth it with a north roof, heavy shading, very low use, or if you'll move house in a few years.
