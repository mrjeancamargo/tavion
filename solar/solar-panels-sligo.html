Solar panels in Sligo

Solar panels in Sligo: how much they make, what you'd save and how long they take to pay back – with the SEAI grant.

PVGIS (EU JRC) · SEAI grant · November 2026

Key facts

  • Sligo gets about 849 kWh per kWp a year on a south-facing roof – 25th of 26 counties, 9% below the Irish average.
  • A 4 kWp system makes about 3,396 kWh a year and costs about €5,200 after the €1,800 grant.
  • With a €250 bill every 2 months it saves about €835 in year 1 and pays back in 6.2 years (7.8 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Over 25 years that's about €15,947 ahead.
  • Best month: May (472 kWh). Worst: December (88 kWh).
