Solar panels in Wicklow

Solar panels in Wicklow: how much they make, what you'd save and how long they take to pay back – with the SEAI grant.

PVGIS (EU JRC) · SEAI grant · November 2026

Key facts

  • Wicklow gets about 1003 kWh per kWp a year on a south-facing roof – 3rd of 26 counties, 7% above the Irish average.
  • A 4 kWp system makes about 4,012 kWh a year and costs about €5,200 after the €1,800 grant.
  • With a €250 bill every 2 months it saves about €964 in year 1 and pays back in 5.4 years (6.9 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Over 25 years that's about €19,160 ahead.
  • Best month: May (528 kWh). Worst: December (128 kWh).
