Tax on solar panel income

Do you pay tax on the money your supplier pays for solar exports? Usually not – here's the limit.

Revenue · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Up to €400 a year of microgeneration income is exempt from income tax, USC and PRSI for 2024 to 2028.
  • Budget 2027 raises this to €600 a year from 2027 (subject to the Finance Act).
  • It's for individuals generating at their sole or main home. Rental properties and businesses don't get it.
  • Under the limit? You don't need to declare it on a tax return.
  • Over the limit? Declare the amount over it – PAYE workers through myAccount (Income Tax Return), self-employed on Form 11.
