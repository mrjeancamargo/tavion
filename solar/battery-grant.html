Solar battery grant (€600)

SEAI now gives €600 towards a home battery. Is it worth adding one to your solar panels?

SEAI · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Flat €600 grant for a battery of 5 kWh or more. Smaller batteries get nothing.
  • Available since 6 October 2026, for homes built and occupied before 2025.
  • Apply with your panels in one application, or on its own for a battery added to existing panels.
  • Must be fitted by an SEAI-registered solar PV company, with the grant offer in place before work starts.
  • A battery stores power you'd otherwise sell for ~18.5c and uses it in the evening instead of buying at ~33c.

A battery makes most sense if…

  • You're out during the day – without a battery you'd sell most of your power cheaply
  • You have a big system (5 kWp+) or lots of summer surplus
  • You're on a night-rate or EV tariff – in winter you can charge the battery cheaply at night (not counted in our numbers)
  • You want some backup power (needs a backup-capable inverter – ask)

…and less sense if

  • You're home all day and already use most of your solar
  • Your system is small (2–3 kWp)
  • Your quote is well over €3,000 for 5 kWh
