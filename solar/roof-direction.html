Which way should solar panels face?

Does your roof have to face south? No – but it makes a difference. Here's how much each direction makes.

PVGIS (EU JRC), midlands · November 2026

Roof facesShallow 15°Standard 35°Steep 50°
South94%100%99%
South-east or south-west90%94%92%
East or west (or split east/west)82%79%74%
North-east or north-west74%63%55%
North71%56%44%

Compared with a south-facing roof at 35° (100%). Panels laid flat on a flat roof make about 84%; most flat-roof installs tilt them, so they do better.

Key facts

  • South is best, but south-east or south-west loses only about 6–7%.
  • East/west loses about 20% – but spreads output across morning and evening, when you're more likely to be home, so you use more of it.
  • North-facing roofs make about 55–70%. On a shallow roof it can still pay; on a steep one it usually doesn't.
  • Shading matters more than direction – a chimney or tree shadow can cut output a lot. Ask about optimisers.
