Solar panels by county

How much solar power each county makes, and how long a typical system takes to pay back.

PVGIS 5.3 (SARAH-3, 2005–2023) · south-facing, 35° · November 2026

Key facts

  • Wexford is the sunniest county for solar (1081 kWh per kWp) and Donegal the least (847).
  • The average is 935 kWh per kWp. The south-east coast does best; the north-west does worst.
  • Even the least sunny county pays back well within the life of the panels.
