2 kWp solar system

How much does a 2 kWp solar system produce in Ireland, what does it cost and how long does it take to pay back?

Key facts

  • A 2 kWp system is about 5 panels of 440 W, needing roughly 10 m² of roof.
  • It makes about 1,694–2,162 kWh a year depending on your county (1,964 kWh in Dublin).
  • Typical price €4,700, SEAI grant €1,400 – you pay about €3,300.
  • In Dublin with a €250 bill it pays back in about 6.2 years (9.1 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Best for a small home or apartment using under 2,500 kWh a year.
