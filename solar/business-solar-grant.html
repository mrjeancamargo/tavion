Business solar grant

Businesses, schools, sports clubs and community groups can get SEAI grants for solar – currently at a boosted rate.

SEAI · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • The Non-Domestic Microgen Grant supports solar PV for businesses, farms, schools, community centres and non-profits – systems up to 1,000 kWp.
  • From 7 October 2026 to 6 July 2027 it's temporarily boosted to 45% of eligible costs, with per-kWp rates doubled.
  • The maximum grant rises from €162,600 to €325,200. You get the lower of the per-kWp amount or 45% of the invoiced cost.
  • Applications made before 7 October 2026 aren't increased.
  • Use an SEAI-registered non-domestic solar PV company and get approval before work starts.

Why act before July 2027

The 45% rate is for new applications for about 9 months. Businesses with high daytime use – shops, offices, cold rooms – usually see the fastest payback. Larger systems need an NC7 or other ESB Networks connection application.
