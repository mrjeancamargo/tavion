What pushes the price up

  • Scaffolding, a high or complex roof, or a split east/west array
  • Premium panels or inverters, optimisers for shading
  • A battery, EV charger or hot-water diverter
  • Upgrading your fuse board or a long cable run

Paying for it

Cash, a Home Energy Upgrade Loan (from about 3–4%), or adding it to your mortgage. Mortgage calculator →
