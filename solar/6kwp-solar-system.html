6 kWp solar system

How much does a 6 kWp solar system produce in Ireland, what does it cost and how long does it take to pay back?

Key facts

  • A 6 kWp system is about 14 panels of 440 W, needing roughly 30 m² of roof.
  • It makes about 5,082–6,486 kWh a year depending on your county (5,892 kWh in Dublin).
  • Typical price €9,300, SEAI grant €1,800 – you pay about €7,500.
  • In Dublin with a €250 bill it pays back in about 5.6 years (6.7 years with a 5 kWh battery).
  • Best for a large home, or one with an EV and heat pump – about the most a standard single-phase house can connect with an NC6.
