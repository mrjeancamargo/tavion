LPT rates by council

Every council's Local Adjustment Factor for Local Property Tax in 2026 and 2027.

Revenue · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Councils can change the basic LPT rate by up to +25% or −15%.
  • For 2027, Cavan, Leitrim and Sligo apply the maximum +25%; Laois +20%. Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown has the biggest cut at −15%.
  • Dublin City, Louth and Meath charge the basic rate (0%).
