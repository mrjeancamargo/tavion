Key facts

  • Carlow County Council's Local Adjustment Factor is +15% for 2027 (no change).
  • A home in band 3 (€315,001 – €420,000) pays €383 a year in 2027.
  • A home in band 1 (up to €240,000) pays €109 a year in 2027.
