LPT on homes over €2.1 million

Homes worth more than €2.1 million aren't in a band – LPT is a percentage of the declared value.

Key facts

  • 0.0906% on the first €1,260,000
  • 0.25% on the part between €1,260,000 and €2,100,000
  • 0.3% on the part above €2,100,000
  • You must declare the exact market value on your LPT return.
