Stamp duty and LPT FAQ

Questions about stamp duty, buying costs and Local Property Tax in Ireland.

Checked November 2026

How much is stamp duty in Ireland?

1% on residential property up to €1 million, 2% on the part between €1 million and €1.5 million, and 6% above €1.5 million.

Do first-time buyers pay stamp duty?

Yes – Ireland has no first-time buyer exemption from stamp duty.

Is stamp duty charged on VAT for new homes?

No. On a new home, stamp duty is charged on the price excluding the 13.5% VAT.

Who pays stamp duty – the buyer or seller?

The buyer. Your solicitor pays it to Revenue through eStamping after you sign the deed.

Can stamp duty be added to my mortgage?

Not normally – lenders lend against the property price, so stamp duty and fees come from your own savings.

How much does it cost to buy a house in Ireland on top of the price?

Typically 2–3% of the price for stamp duty and fees – about €8,700 on a €350,000 second-hand home – plus at least a 10% deposit.

How much is LPT in 2026?

From €95 a year (homes up to €240,000) to €3,110 (up to €2.1 million) before your council's adjustment of −15% to +25%.

Do I pay LPT in the year I buy?

The owner on 1 November pays the following year's LPT. If you buy after 1 November, the seller is liable for the next year.

Does LPT go up if I extend my home?

No. Your band is based on the value on 1 November 2025 and is fixed until 2030.

No. It's a free, independent tool based on Revenue's published rates. Fees are estimates – get quotes from a solicitor.
