LPT valuation bands 2026–2030

All 19 Local Property Tax bands for 2026 to 2030, with the lowest and highest charge after council adjustments.

Valuation date 1 November 2025 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Your band is set by your home's value on 1 November 2025 and stays the same until 2030 – even if the value changes or you improve the home.
  • Basic charges run from €95 (up to €240,000) to €3,110 (€1,995,001 – €2,100,000).
  • Councils can change the basic rate by −15% to +25% each year.
