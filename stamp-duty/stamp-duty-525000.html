Stamp duty on €525,000

How much stamp duty do you pay on a €525,000 house in Ireland, and what are the total costs of buying?

Checked against Revenue · November 2026

Key facts

  • Stamp duty on a €525,000 second-hand home in Ireland is €5,250 (1% of €525,000).
  • On a €525,000 new home (13.5% VAT included) it's €4,626, because stamp duty is charged on the price excluding VAT (€462,555).
  • Total buying costs are about €10,460 (1.99% of the price). With a 10% deposit you need about €62,960 in cash.
  • Local Property Tax: band 4, basic €428 a year (before your council's adjustment).
