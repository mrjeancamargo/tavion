LPT 2027 questions

When is property tax due for 2027?

It depends on how you pay. Paying in full by card or through An Post or Payzone: by 11 January 2027. Annual debit (ADI): taken on 21 March 2027. Monthly direct debit: the 15th of every month from 15 January 2027. Deduction from salary, pension or welfare: spread over the year from January.

What's the deadline to choose how I pay LPT for 2027?

2 December 2026 if you want to spread your payments over 2027 or pay by annual debit. Choose it in the LPT online service on revenue.ie. If you don't, the method you used last year is usually carried forward.

Can I pay LPT monthly?

Yes – by monthly direct debit (from 15 January 2027, then the 15th of each month), by deduction from your salary, pension or social welfare payment, or by regular cash payments through An Post or Payzone.

How much is LPT in 2027?

It depends on your home's valuation band and your council. Band 1 (up to €240,000) is €95 a year before your council's adjustment. Work out yours →

What happens if I don't pay LPT?

Revenue can deduct it from your salary or pension without your agreement, add interest, and refuse a tax clearance certificate. If you have unpaid LPT from earlier years, pay it straight away.
