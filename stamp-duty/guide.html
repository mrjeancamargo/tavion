Stamp duty and LPT guide

Everything buyers and homeowners need to know about stamp duty and Local Property Tax in Ireland.

Checked November 2026

Stamp duty

  • Residential rates: 1% up to €1m, 2% from €1m to €1.5m, 6% above €1.5m
  • New homes: charged on the price excluding 13.5% VAT
  • Buying 10 or more homes within 12 months: 15%
  • Non-residential property and sites without a building agreement: 7.5%
  • There's no first-time buyer exemption
  • Transfers between spouses or civil partners are exempt
  • A gift of a home is charged on its market value
  • Your solicitor files the return and pays through Revenue's eStamping – late payment means interest and penalties

Other costs when you buy

  • Solicitor: about €3,075 including VAT and outlays (get 3 quotes)
  • Land Registry: about €975 for the title + €175 for the mortgage
  • Searches: about €300
  • Bank valuation: about €185
  • Structural survey: about €500 (recommended for second-hand homes)
  • Mortgage protection and home insurance – required by lenders

Local Property Tax

  • LPT is an annual tax on homes, paid to Revenue and used by your council
  • Your band comes from the value on 1 November 2025 and applies for 2026–2030
  • The person who owns the home on 1 November pays the next year's LPT. Buy in December 2026 and the seller pays LPT for 2027
  • Pay in one go, by direct debit, or spread through salary, pension or social welfare deductions
  • You may be able to defer LPT if your income is low – check Revenue's deferral rules
  • Some homes are exempt, for example homes with pyrite or mica damage that qualify, or a home empty for 12 months because the owner is in long-term care
