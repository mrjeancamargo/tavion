LPT band 7: €735,001 – €840,000

How much Local Property Tax do you pay in band 7 (€735,001 – €840,000)? Your charge in every council for 2027.

Revenue valuation bands 2026–2030 · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • LPT band 7 covers homes valued at €735,001 to €840,000 on 1 November 2025.
  • The basic annual charge is €713 (€59.42 a month) for 2026 to 2030.
  • After your council's adjustment it ranges from €606 (−15%) to €891 (+25%) in 2027.
