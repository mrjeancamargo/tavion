How stamp duty works on a home in Ireland

  1. 1% on the first €1,000,000 of the price.
  2. 2% on the part between €1,000,000 and €1,500,000.
  3. 6% on anything above €1,500,000 (since 2 October 2024).
  4. New homes: stamp duty is charged on the price excluding the 13.5% VAT – so a €454,000 new home pays stamp duty on €400,000.
  5. Who pays: the buyer, through their solicitor, using Revenue's eStamping after the deed is signed – late filing means interest and penalties.

Example: on a €350,000 second-hand home, stamp duty is €3,500 and total buying costs are about €8,710 – plus a 10% deposit of €35,000.
