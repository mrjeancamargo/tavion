Notice period calculator Ireland

How much notice does a landlord or tenant have to give in Ireland? Enter the tenancy dates and the reason to see the notice period, the earliest end date and whether the reason is allowed under the March 2026 rules.

  • Landlord: 90 to 224 days
  • Tenant: 28 to 112 days

Shorter notice: 28 days for rent arrears or other breaches (after a valid warning notice), 7 days for serious anti-social behaviour.

Shorter notice: 28 days if the landlord breaches their obligations and doesn't fix it, 7 days if there's a danger to life or the structure. Student specific accommodation: 28 days.

Is the notice valid? Checklist

  • In writing (letter or email) – not a text or a phone call
  • Signed by the landlord or their agent (or by the tenant)
  • Shows the date it is served
  • States the reason (always for landlords; for tenants only if leaving because of a breach or danger)
  • Gives the termination date – with at least the right notice period
  • Says the other side has 90 days (no breach) or 28 days (breach) to refer a dispute to the RTB
  • Landlords: sent to the tenant and the RTB on the same day (since 1 March 2026), using the right RTB template
  • Landlords: includes the statutory declaration or statement required for the reason
Received a notice you think is invalid? You can refer a dispute to the RTB – within 90 days if the notice isn't for a breach, 28 days if it is. Free advice: Threshold, 1800 454 454.
