Rent increase rules in Kildare town

Rent increase rules and notice periods for Kildare town, Co. Kildare: how much your rent can go up, when, and how much notice is needed to end a tenancy.

Updated for the 1 March 2026 rules · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Kildare town was already a Rent Pressure Zone before 20 June 2025.
  • Rent can be reviewed once every 12 months.
  • The rent can go up by at most 2% a year (or CPI inflation if lower – inflation is 4.1% now), with 90 days' written notice copied to the RTB.
  • Example: €1,500 a month last set 12 months ago → maximum €1,537.40, from 8 February 2027 at the earliest (notice served today).

Ending a tenancy in Kildare town

Notice periods are the same everywhere in Ireland. A landlord must give 90 days (under 6 months) up to 224 days (8+ years); a tenant 28 to 112 days. For tenancies created from 1 March 2026, landlords with 4 or more tenancies can only end a tenancy for a tenant breach or if the property no longer suits the tenant. Check a notice →
