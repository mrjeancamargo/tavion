Rent Pressure Zones in Ireland

Is your area a Rent Pressure Zone? Since 20 June 2025 the whole of Ireland is. What still matters is whether your area was an RPZ before that date.

Checked against the Housing Agency and RTB · November 2026

Key facts

  • Every county in Ireland has been a Rent Pressure Zone since 20 June 2025, and from 1 March 2026 a national rent control system covers all private tenancies.
  • Rent can rise by at most 2% a year, or CPI inflation if lower.
  • Areas newly designated on 20 June 2025: for existing tenancies, the first rent review must wait 24 months from when the rent was last set. After that, every 12 months.
  • Areas designated before 20 June 2025 (all of Dublin, Kildare, Meath, Wicklow, Louth, Galway, Kilkenny, Limerick, Waterford, Westmeath and parts of other counties): reviews every 12 months.
