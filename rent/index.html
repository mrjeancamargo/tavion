How rent increases work in Ireland

  1. Once every 12 months. Rent can only be reviewed once a year – or after 24 months for the first review in an area that became a Rent Pressure Zone on 20 June 2025.
  2. 2% a year, or inflation if lower. Since 1 March 2026 the cap uses the Consumer Price Index (CPI). Inflation is 4.1% (September 2026), so the 2% cap applies, pro rata for the time since the rent was last set.
  3. 90 days' written notice. The landlord must send the rent review notice to the tenant and the RTB on the same day, with the RTB Rent Calculator printout and 3 comparable rents from the RTB Rent Register.
  4. No resets for existing tenancies. Tenancies that started before 1 March 2026 can never be reset to market rent. Newer tenancies can reset at the start of the tenancy (in some cases) and at the end of each 6-year cycle.

Example: rent of €1,500 last set 12 months ago in Dublin can rise to at most €1,537.40 (+€37.40), from 8 February 2027 at the earliest if notice is served today.
