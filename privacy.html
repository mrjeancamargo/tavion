Privacy & cookies

This policy covers tavion.ie and the free apps hosted on it, such as the Theory Test Practice. The site is run by TAVION LIMITED, Longford, Ireland.

No accounts, no personal data collected

Our apps don't ask for your name, email or any account. Anything an app needs to remember while you use it (for example, your answers during a mock exam) is kept only in your own browser and is deleted when you close the tab. Your individual answers are never sent to us — only anonymous usage statistics (see Analytics below).

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages and apps are used, so we can improve them and decide what to build next. It records things like pages visited, buttons clicked, test scores (with no names attached), device type and approximate country. Visitors in the EU and UK are asked for consent first, and nothing is stored on your device unless you agree. Learn more in Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

Our apps are free because they show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance. Where the law requires it, you'll be asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services, or manage personalised ads in Google Ad Settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and to keep the service running.
