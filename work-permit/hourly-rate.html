Work permit minimum hourly rates

Work permit minimums are yearly amounts. Here's the hourly pay you need to reach them, depending on your hours.

Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • The rule is the annual minimum. DETE's figures assume a 39-hour week.
  • On 39 hours: GEP €18.05 an hour, Critical Skills €20.17, care roles €16.12.
  • If you work fewer hours, you need a higher hourly rate to reach the same yearly amount.
  • Overtime doesn't count, so working extra hours won't get you over the line – your basic pay must.
