Work permit minimum for care and agri-food workers

Healthcare assistants, home carers, care workers, meat processing and horticulture operatives have a lower employment permit minimum – but it's rising faster than the others.

DETE · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Minimum from 1 March 2026: €32,691 a year (was €30,000) – a 9% rise.
  • DETE also sets a minimum hourly rate of €16.12 for these roles.
  • These are General Employment Permits, so the normal GEP rules apply (Labour Market Needs Test, 9-month rule, renewals).
  • The lower rate is being phased out by 2030, when these roles will need the full GEP minimum (now €36,605).
  • In 2027 the lower rate is expected to move to about 92.5% of the GEP minimum, up from about 89% now.
