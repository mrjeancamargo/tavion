Is €30,000 enough for a work permit?

Is €30,000 enough for an Irish work permit? Here's every permit type against the minimums from 1 March 2026.

DETE thresholds · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • On €30,000 a year you meet the minimum for: none of the permits.
  • General Employment Permit: ❌ €6,605 short of €36,605.
  • Critical Skills: ❌ €10,904 short of €40,904.
  • That's €2,500 a month, or about €14.79 an hour on a 39-hour week.
