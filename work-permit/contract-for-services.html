Contract for Services permit: salary and rules

The Contract for Services permit is for staff of a foreign company that has won a contract to provide services to an Irish business.

DETE · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Minimum: €49,523 a year (was €46,000).
  • Basic salary must be at least the National Minimum Wage. Board and accommodation and health insurance paid by the contractor can count towards the minimum.
  • You must have worked for the contractor for at least 6 months. Subcontracted work isn't eligible.
  • Permit for the contract period, up to 2 years at first, maximum 5 years in total. It doesn't give long-term residency rights.
  • Fees: €1,000 new (6–24 months), €1,500 renewal (6–36 months).
