Critical Skills Employment Permit: salary and rules

The Critical Skills Employment Permit (CSEP) is for jobs in short supply. It has a higher minimum salary but a faster route to Stamp 4 and family reunification.

DETE · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Minimum with a relevant degree for a job on the Critical Skills Occupations List: €40,904 (was €38,000).
  • Recent graduate (relevant degree in the last 12 months): €36,848.
  • Any other eligible job (not on the Ineligible List) paying €68,911 or more (was €64,000). Without a degree you need relevant experience.
  • The job offer must be for at least 2 years. No Labour Market Needs Test is needed.
  • After 2 years you can apply to the Department of Justice for Stamp 4 – no permit renewal needed.
  • Your spouse or partner can join you straight away and can work without an employment permit.

Good to know

  • Public sector roles follow the pay rate in the public sector pay agreement instead.
  • Only basic salary and employer-paid health insurance count – not bonuses. What counts →
  • The fee is €1,000 (90% refunded if refused). Apply at least 12 weeks before your start date.
  • You're expected to stay with your first employer for at least 9 months. Changing employer →
  • Time on Stamp 1 and Stamp 4 counts towards citizenship. Stamp 4 and citizenship →
