Employment permit minimum salaries 2026

Every Irish employment permit minimum salary from 1 March 2026, what it was before, and what it means per hour and per month.

DETE · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • General Employment Permit: €36,605 (was €34,000) – €34,009 for recent Irish graduates.
  • Critical Skills with a relevant degree: €40,904 (was €38,000) – €36,848 for recent graduates.
  • Critical Skills without a relevant degree: €68,911 (was €64,000).
  • Intra-Company Transfer and Contract for Services: €49,523 (was €46,000). ICT trainees: €36,605.
  • Healthcare assistants, home carers, care workers, meat processing and horticulture operatives: €32,691 (was €30,000) – €16.12 an hour.
  • Most thresholds rose by about 7.66%. They apply to new applications and renewals made on or after 1 March 2026.
