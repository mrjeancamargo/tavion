Work permit salary increases in 2027

Will work permit salary minimums go up again in 2027? Here's what the Government has said, and a rough idea of the numbers.

DETE roadmap · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • From 2027 to 2030, the minimums will be reviewed every year in line with changes in average weekly earnings published by the CSO.
  • No 2027 figures have been officially announced yet.
  • Average weekly earnings have risen by about 4.9% a year on average since 2020 (between 3.1% and 7.6%).
  • The lower rate for care and agri-food roles is being phased out by 2030. It's expected to reach about 92.5% of the GEP rate in 2027.
  • Expect any increase to apply to renewals as well as new applications.
