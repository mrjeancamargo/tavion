What you need to know

  1. The minimums apply to new permits and renewals made on or after 1 March 2026 – even if your first permit was granted at the old rate.
  2. Only basic salary and employer-paid health insurance count. Bonuses, overtime and most allowances don't. What counts →
  3. Recent graduates get a lower rate for GEP and Critical Skills permits. Graduate rates →
  4. More increases are coming. From 2027 the minimums are reviewed every year in line with average earnings. What's planned →
