Renewing your work permit

Renewing your work permit after 1 March 2026? Your pay must meet the new minimum – even if your first permit was granted at the old rate.

DETE · Checked November 2026

Key facts

  • Renewals made on or after 1 March 2026 must meet the new minimums – for example €36,605 for a GEP (was €34,000).
  • Apply to renew within 4 months before your permit expires, on Employment Permits Online.
  • GEP renewals are for up to 3 years. After 5 years with the same employer, a renewal can have no fee and no end date.
  • Critical Skills holders don't renew – after 2 years they apply for Stamp 4.
  • Your immigration permission (IRP) is separate – renew it at least a month before it expires.
  • Graduate rate at first? At renewal you'll normally need the standard rate.

Renewal checklist

  • Check your basic pay against the new minimum (use the checker)
  • If you're short, talk to your employer early – the pay rise must be in place and on your payslips
  • Gather recent payslips showing basic pay clearly
  • Apply within the 4-month window
  • Renew your IRP registration with ISD as well
  • Keep everything continuous – a gap in permission breaks your citizenship residence count
